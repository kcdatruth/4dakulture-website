THE 4DK PLAYOFF RACE DESK.

The AFC: Buffalo controls the top line at 6–1, but Denver’s head-to-head win over Kansas City is the real change. Pittsburgh leads a crowded AFC North where Cincinnati and Cleveland still have legitimate wild-card cases. Jacksonville has separated from the rest of the South. The seventh-place line currently belongs to New England at 5–3, with Cleveland also 5–3 but eighth under the league’s standings order. Baltimore’s quarterback injuries could change the race quickly.

The NFC: San Francisco remains first at 6–1, with Minnesota also 6–1 after its first loss. The NFC North is a four-team collision: Chicago 6–2, Detroit 5–2 and Green Bay 5–3 all remain dangerous. The Giants lead the East at 4–2–1. Carolina leads the South at only 3–4, and that is why Tampa Bay and Atlanta still have hope despite losing records. Seattle’s MNF loss dropped it to 4–4 and outside the current top seven, while the Rams are also 4–4 and gaining momentum.

4DK outlook: A projected seed is not an official seed. No playoff ticket is punched in early November. The standings tell us who has leverage; the next month will tell us who can keep it.
