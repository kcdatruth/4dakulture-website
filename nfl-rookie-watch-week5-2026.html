POST-WEEK 5 • 4DK EDITORIAL TOP 10

Week 5 Rookie Watch

Ten names, new movement, and Week 5 context. Our rookie watch is an editorial ranking, not a claim that every individual statistic is verified.

MVP TOP 10ROOKIE WATCHPOWER RANKINGSSTANDINGS

Our post-Week 5 editorial board weighs season-long influence, Sunday impact and opportunity. Verified game figures are identified below; we do not invent missing individual statistics.

1

David Bailey

New York Jets · EDGE

3 sacks, 7 tackles, 3 tackles for loss, 4 QB hits

The rookie changed the Browns game almost single-handedly. His burst and finishing power wrecked protection, and nine pressures put him in rare company. A defensive rookie making that kind of impact deserves the top spot.

2

Sonny Styles

Washington Commanders · LB

13 tackles, 1 TFL, 1 pass defended; forced a crucial overtime fumble

A legitimate sideline-to-sideline difference-maker. The overtime takeaway against the Giants showed why his impact goes beyond tackle volume, even in a tie.

3

Carnell Tate

Tennessee Titans · WR

9 catches, 145 yards in Week 4

The prior breakout keeps Tate high in the race, but Tennessee’s 3-point Week 5 output prevents us from treating the previous game as another fresh explosion. Consistency is the next hurdle.

4

Gavin Bartholomew

Minnesota Vikings · TE

5 catches, 33 yards in Week 5

The numbers are modest, but the context matters. Pressed into a bigger role after T.J. Hockenson went out, he became a reliable outlet in an undefeated team’s win.

5

Willie Lampkin

Philadelphia Eagles · OG

First NFL start; Eagles allowed 1 sack

Not every impactful rookie piles up fantasy numbers. Lampkin handled a difficult assignment in the trenches and gave the Eagles a needed measure of protection.

6

Makai Lemon

Philadelphia Eagles · WR

Week 5 role and production under review

The rookie has flashed talent, but a top-five case needs more than draft reputation. His consistency and involvement are worth tracking.

7

Caleb Douglas

Miami Dolphins · WR

Regular offensive role; individual Week 5 line under review

An emerging contributor on a struggling team. Opportunity is real, but production and week-to-week development will decide how far he rises.

8

Jalon Daniels

Tampa Bay Buccaneers · QB

Week 5 victory over Dallas; individual passing line under review

A young quarterback earning a win deserves attention. The key question is how much of the offense he can own as defenses gather more tape.

9

Anthony Hill Jr.

Tennessee Titans · LB

Individual Week 5 defensive totals not independently confirmed

A holdover from our previous watch list, now evaluated on a fresh board rather than automatically retaining his old No. 2 position.

10

Denzel Boston

Cleveland Browns · WR

Individual Week 5 receiving totals not independently confirmed

The upside remains interesting, but Cleveland’s offensive struggles make his usage and separation worth monitoring.
