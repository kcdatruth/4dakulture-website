WHY THIS EXISTS

GO BACK
WITH CONTEXT.

Kobe's career is usually compressed into rings, 81 points, Mamba Mentality and arguments about where he ranks. The Mamba Files slows it down.

What did the league look like in that moment? What was being asked of him? What changed from No. 8 to No. 24? How dominant were the Shaq–Kobe Lakers really? What did 2005–06 feel like while it was happening?

The point isn't to rewrite the history. It's to remember the history with all the details still attached.

WHICH KOBE WAS BETTER?

No. 8 had more burst, more vertical explosion and three championships before age 24. No. 24 had the deeper bag, the MVP, two titles as the undisputed centerpiece and a more complete command of the game.

This one deserves its own full 4DK case for both sides before we choose a winner.
