Week 7: Unbeaten No More.

San Francisco and Kansas City fall, Minnesota stays perfect, and the playoff picture gets a serious shake-up. Eleven Sunday matchups, all accounted for.

Falcons 29, 49ers 20

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Atlanta handed San Francisco its first loss by forcing four turnovers. Michael Penix Jr. threw three touchdowns; Brock Purdy posted 288 yards and a score but also two interceptions. The Niners led 14–3 before Atlanta ripped off 23 straight points. This is the first true adversity test for a 6–1 team: ball security, second-half adjustments and protection have to be addressed. Christian McCaffrey scored but was held to 39 rushing yards.

Steelers 16, Saints 10

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Pittsburgh controlled the second half in Paris, blanking New Orleans after halftime. Jaylen Warren rushed for 101 yards, while Aaron Rodgers moved into third on the all-time passing-touchdown list with No. 540. The Saints rushed for only 18 yards; Pittsburgh won by dictating the line of scrimmage rather than chasing style points.

Giants 27, Texans 21 (OT)

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

J.J. McCarthy won his Giants starting debut with 252 passing yards. Cam Skattebo ripped off 124 rushing yards, and Devin Singletary ended overtime with a 30-yard touchdown. Malik Nabers added 111 receiving yards. Houston tied the game late through C.J. Stroud but couldn't finish; the Giants' new quarterback situation suddenly looks far more interesting.

Browns 27, Titans 24

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Andre Szmyt hit the walk-off field goal after Tennessee tied it with less than a minute left. Deshaun Watson threw for 308 yards and moved Cleveland into position with a late strike to Harold Fannin Jr. Tony Pollard's three-touchdown effort nearly stole it for Tennessee, but Cleveland found the late-game execution required of a 5–2 contender.

Dolphins 28, Jets 25

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Malik Willis threw two touchdowns to rookie Caleb Douglas and added a rushing score, delivering Miami's first victory. Ollie Gordon II ran for 103 yards. Geno Smith threw three touchdowns for New York, but the Jets couldn't close; the Dolphins finally paired efficient quarterback play with a productive ground game.

Buccaneers 43, Panthers 13

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Bucky Irving ran for 106 yards and two scores, and Tampa Bay's defense turned Carolina's mistakes into a rout. Baker Mayfield returned and completed seven of 11 passes, but the headline was the Buccaneers' dominance on the ground and four Panthers turnovers. Bryce Young's two interceptions and lost fumble made a comeback unrealistic.

Vikings 16, Colts 10

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Minnesota stayed unbeaten at 6–0 behind timely defense and a late Kyler Murray touchdown pass to T.J. Hockenson. Harrison Smith's closing interception protected the lead. Jonathan Taylor rushed for 95 yards and a score for Indianapolis, but the Colts couldn't sustain enough offense to punish the Vikings.

Bengals 35, Ravens 13

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Down 13–0 at halftime, Cincinnati erupted for 35 unanswered points. Joe Burrow threw four second-half touchdowns, with Tee Higgins delivering 148 receiving yards. Baltimore was without Lamar Jackson and squandered another double-digit lead. The Bengals' ceiling remains high whenever Burrow has time and his receivers create separation.

Broncos 30, Cardinals 16

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Bo Nix generated offense with both his arm and legs, including a 62-yard touchdown run. Denver has won three straight and looks increasingly comfortable winning in different ways. Trey McBride caught 10 passes for 111 yards for Arizona, but the Cardinals could not convert that production into enough scoring.

Rams 35, Raiders 21

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Matthew Stafford threw three touchdowns as Los Angeles raced to a 28–3 lead. Kyren Williams and Blake Corum both approached 100 rushing yards and each scored. Kirk Cousins passed for 295 yards and two touchdowns, but the Raiders' ground game produced just 48 yards. Four consecutive losses have turned Las Vegas's promising opening into an urgent course-correction.

Lions 20, Packers 17

4DK WEEK 7 GAME FILE

Jake Bates kicked the winner as time expired. Jared Goff threw for 303 yards and two touchdowns to Jameson Williams, while Amon-Ra St. Brown amassed 153 receiving yards and Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 116. Detroit's balanced attack made the difference in a divisional game that went down to the last possession.
