4DK injury desk · what it means

New York's quarterback and running-back availability matters for its Thursday offensive rhythm. McCarthy and Skattebo improved from limited Monday estimates to full Tuesday participation, an encouraging development for New York. Abdul Carter and Malik Harrison remained limited; Colton Hood was added Tuesday with a finger injury. Washington's most important distinction is the same: Daniels remained listed as a full participant. Burks improved to limited Tuesday, while Lucas Patrick and Colson Yankoff remained non-participants. These practice levels do not establish final game availability. 4DK will not convert coach optimism or secondary trackers into official final designations.

Update policy: Tuesday participation was added without removing Monday figures. Final game statuses will be updated only when officially released; unconfirmed injury claims are not treated as confirmed absences.
