THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Durant is still Durant. At 37 last season he played 78 games, averaged 26 points and shot 52 percent from the field, 41.3 percent from three and 87.4 percent from the line. That level of scoring efficiency at his age is absurd. His release point and footwork mean elite defense often changes only the difficulty of the shot, not whether he can get it off.

Houston is a good environment for this stage of his career. The Rockets have younger defenders, rebounders and creators who can absorb the physical work while Durant handles the possessions that need a bailout scorer. Şengün can create from the middle, Amen can pressure the rim and KD can punish every defense that loads toward them.

The age question is unavoidable. At 38, the drop can arrive quickly, and the playoff load matters more than the regular-season averages. But until the decline actually shows up, ranking one of the most efficient 26-point scorers in the league outside the Top 15 would be harder to defend than putting him here.
