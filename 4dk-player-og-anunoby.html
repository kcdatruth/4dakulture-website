THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

OG is one of the easiest players in basketball to fit next to stars. He guards elite wings, switches onto bigger players, spaces the floor and rarely needs an offense built around him to affect winning. He shot 38.6 percent from three last season and remained a key part of a championship-level Knicks defense. The ranking is lower than his impact because creation matters on a list of individual players, and OG does not carry the same self-created offensive burden as the names above him. But if you are building a contender, there may be ten players ranked ahead of him who are harder to fit.
