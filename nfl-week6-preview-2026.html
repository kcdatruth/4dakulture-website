WEEK 6.
THE PRESSURE
RISES.

Fourteen games. Six divisional matchups. A London showdown, a Sunday-night spotlight in Green Bay and an undefeated San Francisco team closing the week at home. We break down every matchup entering Week 6.

MONDAY NIGHT SPOTLIGHT · OCTOBER 19

5–0 IS THE START. WHAT COMES NEXT?

San Francisco earned its biggest road statement in Seattle. Now comes a different test: handling expectations. The 49ers host Washington, with Brock Purdy’s poise and Mike Evans’ contested-catch ability giving the offense answers in high-leverage moments. But a winning streak is only as good as the next performance. Protect the quarterback, manage Christian McCaffrey’s workload, and stay healthy for the long haul.

EVERY GAME. EVERY ANGLE.

THURSDAY · OCT 15 · 5:15 PM PT

Seattle Seahawks at Denver Broncos

Seattle’s response to the loss against San Francisco is the opening storyline. Denver can turn this into a field-position fight, but Seattle needs to establish offensive rhythm early and avoid letting the pass rush dictate the night.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 6:30 AM PT · LONDON

Houston Texans at Jacksonville Jaguars

An AFC South game with divisional consequences on neutral ground. Jacksonville enters 4–1; Houston needs to make this a physical, possession-by-possession contest. The early kickoff and travel preparation are real variables.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 10:00 AM PT

Chicago Bears at Atlanta Falcons

Both clubs have quarterback health questions entering the week. Atlanta must decide how much offense to run through Bijan Robinson if Michael Penix Jr. is limited; Chicago needs better quarterback stability after the Green Bay loss.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 10:00 AM PT

Baltimore Ravens at Cleveland Browns

Baltimore comes off an overtime win, but Lamar Jackson’s availability is a central question. Cleveland can challenge the Ravens physically; the swing factor is whether Baltimore can create explosive plays without giving away field position.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 10:00 AM PT

Tennessee Titans at Indianapolis Colts

A division game where first-down execution matters. Indianapolis needs to avoid obvious passing situations; Tennessee needs to prove it can sustain drives rather than relying on isolated big plays.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 10:00 AM PT

New York Jets at New England Patriots

New England has momentum after beating Las Vegas. The Jets need their defense to shorten the field and keep the game manageable; this is also a useful measuring stick for New England’s passing-game growth.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 10:00 AM PT

New Orleans Saints at New York Giants

New York’s tie with Washington left unfinished business. The Giants must clean up late-game execution, while New Orleans needs to generate offense consistently enough to avoid another one-possession scramble.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 10:00 AM PT

Carolina Panthers at Philadelphia Eagles

Philadelphia has to reset after its narrow loss in London. Carolina comes off a bye and has a chance to test the Eagles’ discipline and pass protection. Turnovers and red-zone finishing could decide it.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 10:00 AM PT

Pittsburgh Steelers at Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Two teams with postseason aspirations meet in a game likely to hinge on protection and third downs. Tampa Bay needs a cleaner response after the Dallas win; Pittsburgh needs enough offensive consistency to support its defense.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 1:05 PM PT

Arizona Cardinals at Los Angeles Rams

The Rams have a short turnaround after Monday night’s overtime loss in Buffalo. Arizona’s path is to keep the game close into the second half and force Los Angeles to sustain drives without mistakes.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 1:25 PM PT

Los Angeles Chargers at Kansas City Chiefs

Kansas City returns from its bye unbeaten, while the Chargers are searching for their first win. The Chiefs’ advantage is clear on paper, but divisional familiarity makes protection and turnovers worth watching.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 1:25 PM PT

Buffalo Bills at Las Vegas Raiders

Josh Allen brings Buffalo into Allegiant Stadium after the dramatic overtime win against the Rams. Las Vegas needs a much better defensive performance after its loss in New England; controlling the pace is essential.

SUNDAY · OCT 18 · 5:20 PM PT

Dallas Cowboys at Green Bay Packers

Sunday night puts Dallas under a bright light after the Tampa Bay loss. Green Bay’s win over Chicago gives the Packers momentum, but Dallas has the personnel to make this a genuine test if its defense responds.

MONDAY · OCT 19 · 5:15 PM PT

Washington Commanders at San Francisco 49ers

The 5–0 49ers return home after winning in Seattle. Brock Purdy’s composure and his growing connection with Mike Evans are major 4DK storylines. Washington gets another high-pressure test after tying the Giants. San Francisco must protect its health and avoid a letdown.
