4DK SUNDAY NFL PREVIEW • WEEK 2

THE LEAGUE
GETS REAL.

Week 1 introduced the storylines. Week 2 starts testing which ones are real. Fourteen Sunday games, divisional pressure, injury questions and a Sunday night stage in Kansas City.

INJURY NOTE

Practice participation below reflects the latest official reports available Friday morning, Sept. 18. Final game-status designations can still change before kickoff.

1:00 PM ET

THE EARLY WINDOW

FOX • NASHVILLE

EAGLES (1–0) @ TITANS (0–1)

1:00 ET

Philadelphia survived Washington 24–22 with Jalen Hurts throwing three touchdowns. Tennessee needs a cleaner second game from Cam Ward after the Jets held the Titans to 10 points. The pressure point is simple: can Ward stay composed against a defense built to make young quarterbacks speed up?

PLAYERS TO WATCHJalen Hurts • Dallas Goedert • Cam Ward • Anthony Hill Jr.
INJURY WATCHJalen Carter, Cooper DeJean and Jonathan Greenard were limited; Andrew Mukuba did not practice. Tennessee's James Williams was limited.
CBS • FOXBOROUGH

STEELERS (1–0) @ PATRIOTS (0–1)

1:00 ET

Old-school names, new stakes. Aaron Rodgers and Mike McCarthy take Pittsburgh into Foxborough against a Patriots team trying to settle Drake Maye after three fourth-quarter interceptions in the opener. Pittsburgh's pass rush versus Maye's mobility is the chess match.

PLAYERS TO WATCHAaron Rodgers • T.J. Watt • Drake Maye • Rhamondre Stevenson
INJURY WATCHCameron Heyward, Michael Pittman and Darnell Washington missed Thursday practice. Joey Porter Jr. was limited. Patriots CB Carlton Davis III did not practice.
FOX • CHICAGO

VIKINGS (1–0) @ BEARS (1–0)

1:00 ET

Chicago just dropped 59 in Carolina and Caleb Williams accounted for four touchdowns. Minnesota already has a division win, but QB Kyler Murray entered the week in concussion protocol. This is the first real test of whether Chicago's Week 1 explosion was a one-day avalanche or the start of something.

PLAYERS TO WATCHCaleb Williams • D'Andre Swift • Kyler Murray • Jauan Jennings
INJURY WATCHMurray was limited Thursday; Brian O'Neill did not practice. Swift was limited and Bears G Joe Thuney did not practice.
FOX • ATLANTA

PANTHERS (0–1) @ FALCONS (0–1)

1:00 ET

Both teams need a reset. Carolina gave up 59 in Week 1; Atlanta lost a lower-scoring game to Pittsburgh. That makes this less about style points and more about avoiding an 0–2 hole before the season really gets moving.

PLAYERS TO WATCHDarren Waller • Jalen Coker • A.J. Terrell • Chris Lindstrom
INJURY WATCHPanthers DT Bobby Brown III did not practice Thursday. Atlanta had Za'Darius Smith, Tua Tagovailoa and Jawaan Taylor listed as DNP; Chris Lindstrom was limited.
FOX • NEW YORK

PACKERS (0–1) @ JETS (1–0)

1:00 ET

The Jets controlled Tennessee on the ground and now get a Green Bay team coming off a 39–22 loss in Minnesota. New York's young defensive speed can turn this ugly if the Packers' protection problems carry over.

PLAYERS TO WATCHDavid Bailey • Kenyon Sadiq • Green Bay's interior front
INJURY WATCHJets S Minkah Fitzpatrick and EDGE Joseph Ossai did not practice Thursday. Packers DT Javon Hargrave and Warren Brinson also did not practice.
CBS • BALTIMORE

SAINTS (0–1) @ RAVENS (1–0)

1:00 ET

New Orleans pushed Detroit into overtime and nearly stole one. Baltimore looked like a machine in Indianapolis. The Saints have to survive Lamar Jackson's movement and Derrick Henry's power without letting the game turn into a track meet.

PLAYERS TO WATCHLamar Jackson • Derrick Henry • Zay Flowers
INJURY WATCHBaltimore entered Friday with Zay Flowers, Ronnie Stanley and John Simpson among the key offensive injury concerns.
CBS • HOUSTON

BENGALS (1–0) @ TEXANS (0–1)

1:00 ET

Cincinnati's line allowed the lowest pressure rate in the league in Week 1. Now it gets Will Anderson Jr. and the Houston front. On the other side, David Montgomery scored three times against Buffalo, so the Bengals cannot treat the Texans like an 0–1 team with no answers.

PLAYERS TO WATCHJoe Burrow • Amarius Mims • Will Anderson Jr. • David Montgomery
INJURY WATCHBurrow was limited with a back issue. Nico Collins did not practice Thursday; Jadeveon Clowney was also a DNP.
CBS • TAMPA

BROWNS (0–1) @ BUCCANEERS (0–1)

1:00 ET

Somebody is leaving 0–2. Cleveland's new era opened with a 34–10 loss in Jacksonville. Tampa lost a shootout in Cincinnati and now has to clean up its turnovers while keeping Deshaun Watson from extending plays outside structure.

PLAYERS TO WATCHDeshaun Watson • Jerry Jeudy • Tampa's front seven
INJURY WATCHBrowns G Teven Jenkins and CB Tyson Campbell did not practice Thursday. Bucs S Miles Killebrew and CB Jacob Parrish were limited.
4:05 / 4:25 PM ET

THE LATE WINDOW

CBS • DENVER

JAGUARS (1–0) @ BRONCOS (0–1)

4:05 ET

Trevor Lawrence opened with four touchdowns and no interceptions. Denver got punched 31–10 in Kansas City. Last season Jacksonville was the only team to win in Denver during the regular season, so this already has revenge energy for the Broncos.

PLAYERS TO WATCHTrevor Lawrence • Brian Thomas Jr. • Bo Nix • Marvin Mims Jr.
INJURY WATCHBrian Thomas Jr. and Jakobi Meyers were limited for Jacksonville. Marvin Mims Jr. did not practice Thursday for Denver.
CBS • LOS ANGELES

RAIDERS (1–0) @ CHARGERS (0–1)

4:05 ET

Vegas opened with a road win. The Chargers opened Mike McDaniel's offense with one good drive and not enough after that. AFC West games get personal fast, and this one is already about whether Los Angeles can protect Justin Herbert long enough to create explosives.

PLAYERS TO WATCHAshton Jeanty • Brock Bowers • Justin Herbert • Tuli Tuipulotu
INJURY WATCHBowers did not practice Wednesday or Thursday with a knee issue. Chargers WR Ladd McConkey and DB Elijah Molden also missed both practices.
FOX • ARIZONA

SEAHAWKS (1–0) @ CARDINALS (1–0)

4:25 ET

Seattle won its opener but lost Sam Darnold for Week 2, so Drew Lock gets the start. Arizona already went into Los Angeles and beat the Chargers. A division game with a backup quarterback changes the math completely.

PLAYERS TO WATCHDrew Lock • Cooper Kupp • Trey McBride • Budda Baker
INJURY WATCHDarnold is OUT. Cooper Kupp returned to full participation Thursday. Cardinals DT Roy Lopez did not practice; Trey McBride and Budda Baker were full.
FOX • SANTA CLARA

DOLPHINS (0–1) @ 49ERS (1–0)

4:25 ET

San Francisco came home from Melbourne 1–0 after beating the Rams 27–7. Miami is trying to recover from a 27–13 loss in Vegas with Malik Willis running the offense. If the 49ers control the line of scrimmage the way they did in Week 1, Miami could spend the whole afternoon playing from behind.

PLAYERS TO WATCHBrock Purdy • Christian McCaffrey • Nick Bosa • Malik Willis
INJURY WATCHBosa and George Kittle were limited Thursday; McCaffrey and Trent Williams were full. Miami EDGE Chop Robinson did not practice.
FOX • DALLAS

COMMANDERS (0–1) @ COWBOYS (0–1)

4:25 ET

Two NFC East teams already staring at real pressure. Washington lost by two in Philadelphia. Dallas lost at night to the Giants. Dak Prescott called the home opener a game Dallas has to win. The loser starts 0–2 with both losses inside the conference conversation.

PLAYERS TO WATCHJayden Daniels • Dak Prescott • Caleb Downs
INJURY WATCHWashington LB Frankie Luvu did not practice Thursday. Dallas S Malik Hooker and LB DeMarvion Overshown also did not practice.
SUNDAY NIGHT FOOTBALL

PRIME TIME

NBC • KANSAS CITY

COLTS (0–1) @ CHIEFS (1–0)

8:20 ET

Kansas City is back on the big stage six days after beating Denver 31–10. Kenneth Walker III gave the offense a different gear on Monday night, and now the Colts have to solve him while also dealing with Patrick Mahomes. Indianapolis needs a cleaner response after Baltimore ran through it in Week 1.

PLAYERS TO WATCHPatrick Mahomes • Kenneth Walker III • Anthony Richardson • Jonathan Taylor
INJURY WATCHMahomes and Rashee Rice were full participants Thursday; Chris Jones was limited. Colts WR Ashton Dulin has been ruled OUT, while Alec Pierce and DJ Giddens did not practice Thursday.
