The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Mitchell is still one of the safest bets in basketball for star-level scoring. He averaged 27.9 points last season on 48.3 percent shooting and 36.4 percent from three, while continuing to create explosive scoring runs that can change a game in five minutes. The pull-up three forces defenders to chase over screens, and once he gets a shoulder downhill he is strong enough to finish through contact.

Cleveland’s roster has changed around him, but the basic responsibility has not: Mitchell is the player who has to create something when structure breaks down. Harden can organize offense and Mobley can raise the defensive ceiling, yet the late-clock possessions will still find Mitchell.

The question is how far his scoring can carry a team in the postseason. He has produced huge playoff games before, but to climb toward the Top 10 he needs another run where the efficiency holds deep into May. The talent is there. The résumé needs one more level.
