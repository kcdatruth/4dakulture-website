THURSDAY · OCT 29 · 8:15 PM ET

Carolina Panthers AT Green Bay Packers

A short-week test of identity. Green Bay needs a response after the Detroit loss; Carolina has to prove it can hold up against a passing attack that can stress its coverage at every level.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Baltimore Ravens AT Buffalo Bills

The stakes are bigger than one afternoon. Buffalo comes off its bye with Josh Allen in control; Baltimore’s ceiling changes dramatically if Lamar Jackson can return. His status must be checked before kickoff.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Tennessee Titans AT Cincinnati Bengals

Cincinnati cannot waste a home opportunity in a crowded AFC race. Tennessee needs to create pressure and shorten the game rather than allow a shootout.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Arizona Cardinals AT Dallas Cowboys

Dallas needs to turn its Week 7 win over Philadelphia into sustained momentum. Arizona has to limit explosive plays and keep the Cowboys from dictating tempo.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Minnesota Vikings AT Detroit Lions

The last unbeaten team faces a divisional measuring stick. Detroit has the physicality to challenge Minnesota; the Vikings must show their winning formula travels into a hostile NFC North matchup.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Indianapolis Colts AT Jacksonville Jaguars

Jacksonville has a chance to strengthen its AFC South position. Indianapolis needs efficient early downs and a defense capable of preventing long drives.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Las Vegas Raiders AT New York Jets

Both teams need a cleaner offensive identity. Field position, turnovers and third-down execution may decide which side can finally control a full game.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Cleveland Browns AT Pittsburgh Steelers

An AFC North fight built around physicality. Cleveland has already shown it can challenge Pittsburgh; the rematch is about protecting the ball and surviving pressure.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 1:00 PM ET

Atlanta Falcons AT Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Atlanta’s upset of San Francisco raises the standard. Tampa Bay’s offense exploded in Week 7, making this a major test of whether the Falcons can carry their defensive discipline forward.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 4:05 PM ET

Los Angeles Chargers AT Los Angeles Rams

The Rams enter with momentum after beating Las Vegas. The Chargers need to disrupt Matthew Stafford’s timing and avoid giving the Rams easy early scoring chances.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 4:25 PM ET

Kansas City Chiefs AT Denver Broncos

The AFC West race is on the line. Kansas City must answer after Seattle held it to three points; Denver brings a three-game winning streak and a defense that can turn protection problems into game-changing plays.

SUNDAY · NOV 1 · 4:25 PM ET

New England Patriots AT Miami Dolphins

New England needs to establish its preferred pace; Miami comes off its first win and needs to show that improvement is sustainable rather than a one-week spike.

SUNDAY NIGHT · NOV 1 · 8:20 PM ET

Philadelphia Eagles AT Washington Commanders

Philadelphia has to respond to a difficult Monday night loss to Dallas. Washington has an opportunity to make the division race more complicated with a home win.

MONDAY · NOV 2 · 8:15 PM ET

Chicago Bears AT Seattle Seahawks

A statement game for both sides. Caleb Williams and Chicago’s explosive attack meet the Seattle defense that just held Kansas City to three points. Jaxon Smith-Njigba gives Seattle the kind of receiver who can change the geometry of a defense.
