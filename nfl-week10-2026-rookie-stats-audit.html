November 10 cross-source correction: NFL.com currently displays two conflicting career tables for Mansoor Delane: its standard player statistics page reports 27 combined tackles (17 listed tackles and 10 assists), while its alternate career-stats page reports 26 total tackles (16 solo, 10 assists). The Chiefs' game log header also shows 16 solo tackles. CBS' November 6 report cites 27 tackles. Accordingly, do not mark the combined tackle count as definitively verified. Hold it as 26–27 reported, pending official reconciliation. The sources consistently show 1 interception and 5 passes defended, with five games played.

Availability note: CBS reported on November 6 that Delane was ruled out for the November 8 Jets game with a shoulder injury. This is a historical Week 9 designation, not a Week 10 game-status prediction.
