4 DA KULTURE / NFL DESK / OCTOBER 26, 2026

WEEK 7 MVP WATCH

Our 4DK top ten after Sunday: Allen holds No. 1, Purdy faces his first loss, and Walker gets a defining test. Editorial rankings, not official award odds.

← BACK TO THE NFL DESK
CUTOFF: Sunday, October 25, 2026. Dallas–Philadelphia on Monday night has not been included. Earlier Week 6 rankings are preserved here. These are our editorial judgments, not a claim that all players have identical statistical samples.
01
QB • Buffalo Bills

Josh Allen

The most convincing all-around value case remains the quarterback who can rescue a possession in multiple ways. Allen entered Week 7 with 17 combined passing and rushing touchdowns and a 5–1 Bills team. Buffalo’s defensive volatility only sharpens the question: how many wins would this team have without his improvisation and red-zone creation? His turnover risk keeps this race open.

02
QB • San Francisco 49ers

Brock Purdy

The 49ers finally lost, 29–20 in Atlanta, but one result cannot erase six weeks of outstanding efficiency. Through six games Purdy had 14 passing touchdowns and only two interceptions while navigating a depleted receiving corps. The test now is how he responds when defenses make San Francisco chase a game instead of dictate it.

03
RB • Kansas City Chiefs

Kenneth Walker III

A running back belongs in this conversation when his explosiveness reshapes the offense. Walker’s early-season production powered Kansas City’s unbeaten start, but the 20–3 loss in Seattle interrupts the momentum. We are not dropping him out because of one bad night; we are asking whether his game-changing runs can return when opponents commit to shutting them down.

04
QB • Jacksonville Jaguars

Trevor Lawrence

Lawrence has mixed high-level throws and efficient stretches with costly mistakes. Jacksonville’s competitiveness keeps him relevant, but his case depends on better finishing and fewer giveaways in the games that matter most.

05
QB • Detroit Lions

Jared Goff

Detroit’s 20–17 win over Green Bay reinforces Goff’s place in the conversation. We value quarterbacks who can keep a contender on schedule when the margin is narrow. His ceiling in this race depends on how much of the Lions’ success he drives rather than simply manages.

06
RB • Detroit Lions

Jahmyr Gibbs

Gibbs gives Detroit an explosive counterweight to its passing game. A non-quarterback candidate needs sustained weekly impact, not only highlight runs, and that is the standard we will keep applying.

07
QB • Green Bay Packers

Jordan Love

Green Bay’s close loss in Detroit does not erase Love’s candidacy, but head-to-head divisional games are where an MVP résumé can separate itself. He needs to turn competitive outings into signature victories.

08
QB • Carolina Panthers

Bryce Young

Young entered Week 7 as a genuine riser because of his efficiency against pressure. Tampa Bay’s 43–13 victory over Carolina is a hard reset. We want to see whether he can restore command when his team falls behind and opponents know he must throw.

09
WR • Seattle Seahawks

Jaxon Smith-Njigba

Seattle’s 20–3 win over Kansas City is a team statement. Smith-Njigba’s route craft and ability to create offense against quality defenses keep a receiver in our extended MVP discussion, even though the voting bar at his position is exceptionally high.

10
QB • Houston Texans

C.J. Stroud

Stroud has shown stretches of high-level play, but Houston’s 27–21 loss to the Giants is another reminder that individual value must translate into sustained results. He remains in the top ten on upside and the burden of carrying his offense, not on an untouchable résumé.
