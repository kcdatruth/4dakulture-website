The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Harden is not the one-man scoring system he was in Houston, but the brain is still elite. He averaged 23.6 points and eight assists last season, and few guards are better at manipulating a pick-and-roll, identifying the weakest defender and manufacturing a quality shot late in the clock. Cleveland gives him another chance to organize a talented offense without needing to carry a 35-percent usage rate. Age lowers the ceiling and the defensive value is limited, but his ability to create efficient offense for himself and everybody around him still belongs comfortably inside the Top 40.
