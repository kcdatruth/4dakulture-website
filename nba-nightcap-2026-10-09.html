HOUSTON @ DALLAS • MACAO

Houston Looked Like A Team With Too Many Ways To Hurt You.

135–117

The Rockets led wire to wire and got production everywhere. Tari Eason had 20 and nine, Fred VanVleet 17 with seven assists, Reed Sheppard 16, Kevin Durant 15, and Şengün controlled the middle with a 16-point triple-double.

The encouraging part was that Durant did not have to carry them. Houston moved it, created layers and looked organized. Dallas still got a useful 19-point night from Cooper Flagg in 19 minutes, but Houston's offensive cohesion was the bigger story.

MEMPHIS @ CHICAGO

Memphis Won. Caleb Wilson Made The Loudest Statement.

104–100

Memphis led 60–50 at halftime behind balance from Ty Jerome, GG Jackson, Zach Edey and Cam Boozer. Then Caleb Wilson changed the whole feel of the game.

Wilson had one point in the first half and finished with 21, drilling five threes and scoring 20 after halftime. Norman Powell added 17, but the rookie became Chicago's biggest story again. Memphis still deserves credit for closing it out.
