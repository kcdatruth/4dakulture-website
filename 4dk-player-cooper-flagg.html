THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Flagg already belongs in this conversation after a Rookie of the Year season that confirmed the two-way tools were real immediately. Twenty-one points, nearly seven rebounds and 4.5 assists as a teenager is not normal production, and his value goes beyond the box score because he can defend, cut, handle and create secondary offense. The ranking stays conservative because one NBA season is still one NBA season. Dallas now has to find out how much of the offense can be placed in his hands without dulling the defensive activity that makes him special. His ceiling is dramatically higher than No. 48.
