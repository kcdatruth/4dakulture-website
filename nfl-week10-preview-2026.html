SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 9:30 AM ET

PATRIOTS vs LIONS · MUNICH

The international stage puts two ambitious teams under the microscope. Detroit has to establish its physical identity without letting the early kickoff turn into a sluggish start. New England's challenge is to stay disciplined, create manageable third downs and prevent the Lions from controlling the tempo.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Watch the line of scrimmage: can Detroit generate explosive plays without sacrificing protection, and can New England finish drives rather than settle for field goals?

ENTERING WEEK 10: 5-4 vs. 6-2 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Drake Maye, Jared Goff, Amon-Ra St. Brown

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Drake Maye · Jared Goff · Amon-Ra St. Brown — Detroit enters averaging 27.8 points and 379.8 total yards per game; New England averages 23.4 points and 369.4 yards. The sharper contrast is ball security: the Lions have five giveaways, while the Patriots have 19. Detroit also has 27 sacks to New England's 25. Those figures make Maye's protection and decision-making central to the matchup, while Goff's rhythm and St. Brown's rib status are essential to Detroit's passing plan.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

New England is without TreVeyon Henderson and Carlton Davis; Rhamondre Stevenson and A.J. Brown are questionable. Detroit is without Kerby Joseph, D.J. Reed and center Cade Mays; Amon-Ra St. Brown is questionable.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

The Patriots must manufacture a rushing identity despite Henderson's absence and possible limitations for Stevenson. That puts a larger burden on Maye to keep the offense on schedule, particularly against a Detroit front built to attack obvious passing downs. On the other side, Goff's timing game becomes even more important if Amon-Ra St. Brown is limited. Detroit should test New England's depleted secondary horizontally before looking for explosive shots.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

Detroit's 6-2 start gives this international matchup real NFC seeding weight; New England at 5-4 is fighting to stay above the AFC's crowded middle. The Patriots cannot afford a string of three-and-outs that hands Goff short fields. Detroit needs to prove its offense remains functional through injuries in the middle of the line and back end of its defense.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 1:00 PM ET

CHIEFS at FALCONS

Kansas City arrives with championship expectations and little room for careless football. Atlanta's opportunity is to make this a possession-by-possession fight rather than allow the Chiefs to dictate the game with their passing concepts and situational execution.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Third down and red zone are the swing points. Atlanta must create pressure without giving Kansas City easy answers over the middle.

ENTERING WEEK 10: 6-2 vs. 3-6 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Patrick Mahomes, Kenneth Walker III, James Pearce Jr.

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Patrick Mahomes · Travis Kelce · Kansas City's backfield — Mahomes is the player Atlanta must force into long-yardage situations. Kelce's availability gives Kansas City a trusted middle-of-field answer, while the backfield rotation matters if Kenneth Walker III is limited. The Falcons need their defensive front to disrupt the timing of those connections; James Pearce Jr.'s status therefore matters beyond the injury report.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Chiefs RB Kenneth Walker III is questionable with a groin injury; CB Mansoor Delane is questionable. Atlanta WR Zachariah Branch is out and pass rusher James Pearce Jr. is questionable.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

Kansas City enters scoring 25.3 points per game and averaging 384.1 yards, compared with Atlanta's 20.9 points and 322.7 yards. The Chiefs also own a significant turnover edge: plus-four versus Atlanta's minus-seven. If Walker is limited, the run-game rotation and pass protection become important variables rather than automatic advantages. Atlanta's best chance is to turn this into a game about the Falcons' ground attack and pressure packages instead of asking its passing offense to trade possessions with Mahomes.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

At 6-2, Kansas City is building toward playoff positioning; at 3-6, Atlanta needs a genuine upset to change the direction of its season. A loss for the Chiefs would be a costly missed opportunity against a struggling opponent. For Atlanta, competitive stretches are no longer enough: it needs four-quarter execution and a clean turnover sheet.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 1:00 PM ET

TEXANS at BROWNS

Houston's ceiling depends on protecting its quarterback and finding offensive rhythm against a defense capable of wrecking a game up front. Cleveland has an opportunity to shorten the game and turn field position into a weapon.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Can Houston sustain drives under pressure? Cleveland's front must win early downs to force predictable passing situations.

ENTERING WEEK 10: 3-5 vs. 5-4 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: C.J. Stroud, Will Anderson Jr., Danielle Hunter

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

C.J. Stroud · Cleveland's defensive front — Stroud's ability to diagnose pressure and deliver on schedule will determine whether Houston can survive its personnel losses. Cleveland's front needs to win first down and force longer dropbacks; Houston's receivers must create separation quickly rather than waiting for extended plays.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Houston has ruled out David Montgomery, Marvin Harrison Jr., Danielle Hunter and Azeez Al-Shaair; Will Anderson Jr. is questionable. Cleveland safety Ronnie Hickman Jr. is out.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

Houston's challenge is not simply moving the football; it is doing so with significant losses at the skill positions and on defense. That changes the way the Texans should approach third down. Stroud cannot be asked to repeatedly rescue long-yardage situations when the offense is short-handed. Cleveland should lean into its defensive front and force Houston to earn every conversion. The Browns also have to avoid turning defensive success into wasted possessions on offense.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

Cleveland's 5-4 record gives this game immediate AFC wild-card relevance. Houston at 3-5 is running out of comfortable weeks to regain ground. If the Texans win despite their injury list, it would say something meaningful about their depth; if Cleveland wins, it reinforces the Browns as a legitimate playoff challenger.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 1:00 PM ET

VIKINGS at PACKERS

A classic NFC North measuring stick. Minnesota cannot let one disappointing performance become a trend, while Green Bay has a chance to strengthen its place in a tightly contested division. Familiarity makes this less about surprise and more about execution.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Ball security, explosive plays and late-down defense could decide a rivalry game where every possession carries divisional weight.

ENTERING WEEK 10: 6-2 vs. 6-3 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Kirk Cousins, Jordan Love, Jordan Addison

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Kirk Cousins · Jordan Love · Minnesota's receiving corps — Cousins' first priority is timing with his new offense, particularly with T.J. Hockenson unavailable. Love must navigate an offense without Josh Jacobs, placing extra weight on his pre-snap reads, accuracy and ability to avoid drive-killing negative plays.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Minnesota has ruled out Kyler Murray, Jauan Jennings and T.J. Hockenson; Jordan Addison is questionable. Green Bay is without Josh Jacobs and Micah Parsons; Xavier McKinney is questionable.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

This is the weekend's most consequential division collision. Minnesota has moved to Kirk Cousins after acquiring him at the trade deadline, creating a major test of how quickly a veteran quarterback can synchronize with a new offense. Without Hockenson and potentially with Addison limited, Cousins may need to win with anticipation and disciplined underneath throws. Green Bay has its own structural problems: Jacobs' absence changes short-yardage and red-zone decisions, while losing Parsons reduces its ability to create pressure without blitzing.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

Minnesota at 6-2 and Green Bay at 6-3 are separated by the slimmest of margins. Head-to-head results can matter enormously in the NFC North race and eventual tiebreakers. This isn't just about who looks better on Sunday: it is about which staff can adapt its scheme to missing stars and still dictate terms late in the fourth quarter.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 1:00 PM ET

DOLPHINS at COLTS

Indianapolis needs to turn offensive talent into sustained consistency, while Miami is fighting to establish an identity that travels. The Colts can attack inside and test Miami's tackling; the Dolphins must avoid falling behind the chains.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Who controls the middle of the field, and which team can generate points without relying on one spectacular play?

ENTERING WEEK 10: 1-7 vs. 2-7 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Miami's offensive line, Indianapolis' tight-end rotation

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Miami's interior offensive line · Indianapolis' tight ends — With center Aaron Brewer unavailable, watch Miami's protection calls and snap-to-snap communication. Indianapolis' tight-end absences may force different formations and reduce the easy middle-of-field answers normally available to its quarterback.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Miami center Aaron Brewer is out with a concussion. Indianapolis is without tight ends Mo Alie-Cox and Will Mallory and several defensive contributors.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

Both clubs are in a difficult position, which makes the temptation to treat this as a low-stakes game a mistake. For Miami, losing its starting center threatens communication and interior protection, especially on third-and-long. Indianapolis' depleted tight-end group can change personnel packages and how often the Colts can threaten the seams. Both coaching staffs must find a reliable early-down formula instead of chasing explosive plays from desperate down-and-distance situations.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

At 1-7, Miami's realistic path to the postseason is extremely narrow; at 2-7, Indianapolis is similarly focused on proving the second half of the year can be better than the first. This is an evaluation game for quarterbacks, protection schemes and the supporting cast. A win may not fix the standings, but another mistake-filled loss raises deeper questions about direction.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 1:00 PM ET

PANTHERS at SAINTS

Two NFC South teams with plenty to prove. Carolina needs efficient quarterback play and a credible rushing attack. New Orleans must show it can play a complete game, protect the football and convert opportunities.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Look for the team that wins first down and controls field position; turnovers could be decisive.

ENTERING WEEK 10: 3-5 vs. 3-5 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Carolina's ground game, Travis Etienne

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Carolina's linebackers · Travis Etienne · New Orleans' offensive line — Carolina needs reliable second-level tackling with Bobby Okereke out. New Orleans' depleted offensive line makes Etienne's status important: if he is limited, the Saints may have fewer credible ways to stay ahead of the chains.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Carolina linebacker Bobby Okereke is out; Jonathon Brooks and Mike Jackson are doubtful. New Orleans is without center Erik McCoy and tackle Taliese Fuaga; Travis Etienne is questionable.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

The Saints' offensive-line absences could define the entire game. Without McCoy and Fuaga, communication on stunts and edge protection becomes a major concern, particularly if Etienne cannot shoulder a normal workload. Carolina needs to turn those vulnerabilities into negative plays rather than simply allowing New Orleans to stay on schedule. The Panthers' own backfield questions mean they also need to be efficient in the short passing game and avoid predictable second-and-long sequences.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

Both teams enter 3-5, so the winner stays within reach of a division conversation while the loser slips further behind. The NFC South can create opportunities for teams that get hot late, but neither club can keep relying on hypothetical turnarounds. This is a test of whether either team can play disciplined, complementary football.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 1:00 PM ET

BILLS at JETS

Buffalo enters as one of the AFC's measuring sticks, but division games rarely respect power rankings. The Jets' path is disruption: pressure, takeaways and forcing Buffalo into long-yardage situations.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Josh Allen's decision-making against pressure is the central matchup. Buffalo cannot let an inferior position in the standings become a trap.

ENTERING WEEK 10: 7-1 vs. 2-7 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Josh Allen, Garrett Wilson, Buffalo's offensive tackles

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Josh Allen · Garrett Wilson · Buffalo's offensive tackles — Allen can turn broken plays into first downs, but Buffalo must protect him if Spencer Brown cannot play. Wilson's concussion status determines how much of the Jets' passing game can threaten the Bills outside the numbers.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Buffalo tackle Spencer Brown is doubtful. Jets receiver Garrett Wilson is questionable with a concussion; linebacker Trevin Wallace is out.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

Buffalo has the clearest talent-and-record advantage of the weekend, but an offensive-tackle concern can still affect how aggressively Josh Allen attacks downfield. The Jets need to make Allen hold the ball, then rally to underneath throws without allowing him to escape for first downs. If Wilson cannot play, New York loses a major part of its ability to threaten Buffalo's secondary and may have to manufacture offense through formation and motion rather than isolated matchups.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

At 7-1, Buffalo is playing for the AFC's highest seeds, where even one avoidable divisional loss can matter in January. The Jets are 2-7 and playing for pride, evaluation and a chance to disrupt a rival's path. For the Bills, the standard isn't merely winning: it's demonstrating they can control a game they're expected to control.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 1:00 PM ET

JAGUARS at TITANS

Jacksonville has a chance to reinforce its standing in the AFC race. Tennessee's challenge is to make the Jaguars earn every yard and avoid the self-inflicted mistakes that can turn a close divisional game into a rout.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Jacksonville must protect the ball and stay patient; Tennessee needs explosive moments and a stronger third-down performance.

ENTERING WEEK 10: 6-2 vs. 1-7 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Trevor Lawrence, Carnell Tate

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Trevor Lawrence · Carnell Tate · Tennessee's offensive line — Lawrence's ball security and third-down accuracy should set Jacksonville's floor. Tennessee's loss of Carnell Tate and Peter Skoronski reduces the offense's margin for error; the Titans need contributions from the next receiver and lineman up.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Jacksonville is without BJ Green II, Jordan Davis and Jessie Bates. Tennessee receiver Carnell Tate and guard Peter Skoronski are out; Tyjae Spears is questionable.

THE 4DK DEEP DIVE

Jacksonville has to resist the trap of playing the standings instead of the opponent. Tennessee's offensive-line and receiving absences limit its flexibility, so the Jaguars should attack early downs and force the Titans to string together long drives. Jacksonville's own defensive absences mean that approach requires sound tackling and clean coverage exchanges, not reckless pressure. On offense, Trevor Lawrence must be decisive, protect the ball and capitalize when Tennessee gives him favorable field position.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

Jacksonville at 6-2 has a real opportunity to strengthen its AFC South position. Tennessee at 1-7 is trying to build credible momentum rather than merely stay close. The Jaguars' playoff case gets stronger when they beat vulnerable division opponents; a stumble here would undermine the consistency expected of a contender.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

At 5-4, the Rams are fighting to keep pace in the NFC playoff race, and a divisional win matters disproportionately. Arizona at 3-6 needs to show that its season has more to offer than flashes of competitiveness. The Rams need a clean road performance; the Cardinals need a complete one.

The battle in the trenches and red-zone efficiency will tell us more than the highlight plays. This is a credibility test for both teams.

SUNDAY · WEEK 10 · 8:20 PM ET

STEELERS at BENGALS · SUNDAY NIGHT

AFC North football under the lights: physical, emotional and loaded with implications. Pittsburgh needs to keep its defense aggressive without surrendering explosive gains. Cincinnati must protect its quarterback and stay balanced enough to punish overcommitment.

THE 4DK FILM ROOM KEY

Third downs, protection calls and turnovers will shape this one. The winner gains more than a divisional result: it gains leverage in the AFC race.

ENTERING WEEK 10: 6-3 vs. 5-4 · KEY PLAYERS / UNITS: Joe Burrow, Pittsburgh's defensive front

4DK KEY PLAYERS TO WATCH

Joe Burrow · Pittsburgh's front seven · Cincinnati's protection — Burrow's accuracy against disguised pressure is Cincinnati's clearest offensive advantage. Pittsburgh's rush must make him move without allowing escape lanes; the Bengals' line has to prevent sacks from turning manageable possessions into punts.

THE PERSONNEL REPORT

Check the final game-status report and inactive list before kickoff; protection and defensive-back availability are especially important in this rivalry.

The Steelers must generate disruption without sacrificing coverage leverage against a quarterback capable of punishing isolated defenders. Cincinnati needs to protect Burrow and establish enough of a rushing threat to keep Pittsburgh from living in exotic passing-down looks. The Bengals' biggest danger is letting one sack or turnover create a two-possession hole against a defense that thrives on playing with a lead.

PLAYOFF PICTURE & WHAT IT MEANS FOR BOTH TEAMS

Pittsburgh at 6-3 and Cincinnati at 5-4 make this a genuine AFC North leverage game. A Steelers win creates breathing room; a Bengals win tightens the race and strengthens their case as a postseason threat. Sunday night should tell us which team can execute its identity when the opponent knows exactly what's coming.
