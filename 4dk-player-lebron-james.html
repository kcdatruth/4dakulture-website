The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

The career résumé has nothing to do with No. 21. This list is about right now. LeBron averaged 20.9 points, 6.1 rebounds and 7.2 assists at age 41 last season, which would be absurd for almost any player and is even more absurd this late in a career. He can still manipulate a defense, punish mismatches, quarterback transition and turn a playoff possession into whatever matchup he wants.

Philadelphia changes the workload. With Maxey, Jaylen Brown and Embiid around him, LeBron should not need to carry 2018-level usage. That may actually make him more dangerous. He can conserve possessions, attack second-side mismatches, become a screener, punish switches in the post and spend more energy on the defensive possessions that matter most.

The reason he is not higher is simple: you cannot assume the body will give you superstar impact every night across 82 games. At this stage, the value is about controlling the biggest moments. If Philadelphia reaches May healthy, there may still be fewer than 20 players you would trust more in one playoff game.
