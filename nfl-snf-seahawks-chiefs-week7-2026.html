4DK • OCTOBER 25, 2026 • THE NIGHTCAP

Seattle Silences Kansas City.

Sunday Night Football • A 20–3 defensive statement against the previously unbeaten Chiefs.

Seahawks 20, Chiefs 3

THE 4DK SNF DEEP DIVE

Seattle's defense delivered the defining performance of Week 7. Patrick Mahomes finished 19-of-33 for 165 yards and an interception; Kansas City managed only 213 total yards. Seattle produced 410, with Jaxon Smith-Njigba catching nine passes for 150 yards and a touchdown. Sam Darnold threw for 257 yards and a score, while Emanuel Wilson rushed for 100 yards and iced the game with a 21-yard touchdown. Kenneth Walker III, returning to Seattle in a Chiefs uniform, was held to 36 yards on 14 carries. The Seahawks are 4–3 after a rough stretch, and this was a reminder of the physical, disciplined formula that won them the Super Bowl. For the previously unbeaten Chiefs, 3 points is a warning that even a loaded roster can be neutralized when protection, early-down success and explosive plays disappear.

What this means for Seattle

4–3 • Defending champions

This wasn't simply an upset. Seattle's pass defense limited Mahomes, the run game controlled possessions, and Smith-Njigba gave Darnold a true go-to weapon. If that formula travels, the Seahawks can reassert themselves in the NFC playoff chase.

What this means for Kansas City

5–1 • First defeat

The Chiefs are still among the AFC's strongest teams, but the offensive line, run-game efficiency and downfield answers require scrutiny. Walker's 36 yards in his return underscored Seattle's control of the line of scrimmage. A 17-point loss is one thing; scoring only three points is the bigger concern.
