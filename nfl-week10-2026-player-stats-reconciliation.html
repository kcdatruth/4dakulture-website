4DK editorial implications

Green Bay's passing ceiling: Love's 2,618 yards reinforce why the Packers remain dangerous even when their week-to-week performance swings. Production is not the same thing as consistency, and the playoff analysis must account for both.

Seattle's receiving engine: Smith-Njigba's 1,036 yards and nine receiving touchdowns support his unusually strong case for inclusion in the 4DK MVP Watch despite playing wide receiver. The receiving total alone does not resolve Seattle's broader offensive questions.
