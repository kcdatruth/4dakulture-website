THE 4DK MATCHUP FILES

9:30 AM ET · Madrid

CINCINNATI BENGALS AT ATLANTA FALCONS

A neutral-site kickoff adds an unfamiliar rhythm to the game. Cincinnati's offense has the opportunity to dictate the tempo, but Atlanta cannot afford to let the Bengals turn the opening quarter into a track meet. Watch the Falcons' ability to generate pressure without sacrificing coverage behind it.

1:00 PM ET

DENVER BRONCOS AT CAROLINA PANTHERS

Denver enters with the momentum of a statement win over Kansas City. The question is whether that urgency carries into a road game where the Broncos are expected to set the standard. Carolina needs sustained drives and disciplined protection to prevent Denver from controlling field position.

1:00 PM ET

DALLAS COWBOYS AT INDIANAPOLIS COLTS

Both teams have reason to treat this as a reset. Dallas needs cleaner situational football after another close defeat; Indianapolis must respond after Jacksonville overwhelmed it. The turnover battle and third-down execution will tell us which team actually learned from Week 8.

1:00 PM ET

NEW YORK JETS AT KANSAS CITY CHIEFS

Kansas City has to answer the Denver loss, and a home matchup offers the chance to regain control of its offensive identity. The Jets cannot win by merely hanging around. They need explosive plays and a defense capable of making Patrick Mahomes operate patiently.

1:00 PM ET

DETROIT LIONS AT MIAMI DOLPHINS

Detroit just took down Minnesota, but a road date in Miami asks a different question: can the Lions impose their physical style when the opponent wants speed and space? Miami needs a faster start and fewer wasted possessions to keep this game from becoming a battle on Detroit's terms.

1:00 PM ET

CLEVELAND BROWNS AT NEW ORLEANS SAINTS

Cleveland has an opportunity to reinforce its identity against a Saints team searching for answers. The Browns should prioritize early-down efficiency and pressure; New Orleans must avoid letting negative plays create obvious passing situations.

1:00 PM ET

NEW YORK GIANTS AT PHILADELPHIA EAGLES

A divisional game is rarely as simple as the records suggest. Philadelphia is under pressure to respond to its recent slide, while New York can attack a rival's confidence with an aggressive opening script. Protection, red-zone execution and composure matter more than the headlines.

1:00 PM ET

LOS ANGELES RAMS AT WASHINGTON COMMANDERS

The Rams come off a convincing win over the Chargers and have a chance to keep building. Washington needs to turn possession into points rather than relying on isolated explosive moments. The matchup is a test of which staff can create the more favorable passing situations.

4:05 PM ET

HOUSTON TEXANS AT LOS ANGELES CHARGERS

The Chargers must find an offensive response after being held to nine points by the Rams. Houston's chance is to disrupt the timing of the passing game and force long drives. This could be decided by red-zone efficiency rather than total yardage.

4:25 PM ET

LAS VEGAS RAIDERS AT SAN FRANCISCO 49ERS

For 4DK, this is one of the games of the week. The Raiders bring confidence after winning a high-scoring road game against the Jets; San Francisco must control the line of scrimmage and avoid allowing Las Vegas to turn it into a possession-for-possession shootout. Watch how the 49ers deploy their playmakers early.

4:25 PM ET

GREEN BAY PACKERS AT NEW ENGLAND PATRIOTS

Jordan Love and Drake Maye headline a quarterback matchup with real playoff implications. Green Bay survived Carolina, but the late-game questions have not disappeared. New England will try to make the Packers sustain drives and earn every scoring opportunity.

4:25 PM ET

ARIZONA CARDINALS AT SEATTLE SEAHAWKS

Arizona arrives after a narrow win over Dallas. Seattle needs a response after Monday night's loss to Chicago. The division stakes make this more than a bounce-back game: whoever wins the early-down battles can dictate how aggressively the opposing quarterback must play.

8:20 PM ET · SNF

TAMPA BAY BUCCANEERS AT CHICAGO BEARS

Sunday night's spotlight belongs to Chicago after its impressive win in Seattle. The Bears have to prove they can stack performances against a Tampa Bay team capable of punishing coverage mistakes. Watch Chicago's protection and Tampa Bay's ability to finish drives.

Monday 8:15 PM ET · MNF

BUFFALO BILLS AT MINNESOTA VIKINGS

This is the marquee finish to Week 9. Buffalo's offensive ceiling can overwhelm opponents, but Minnesota has to treat this as an opportunity to reclaim momentum after the Detroit loss. The central question: can the Vikings keep Josh Allen from turning broken plays into backbreaking gains?
