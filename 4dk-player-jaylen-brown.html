The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Brown is coming off one of the best individual regular seasons of his career: 28.7 points, 6.9 rebounds and 5.1 assists while carrying a huge burden in Boston. He has become more comfortable creating through contact, attacking mismatches and making the second pass when defenses send help. The physicality remains the foundation. He can score without needing perfect spacing and defend bigger wings without being protected.

Philadelphia gives him a completely different role. He will not have to be the primary scorer every night with Maxey, Embiid and LeBron around him, which means the best version of Brown may be the one that spends more energy defending and punishing tilted floors. He should get cleaner driving lanes and more opportunities against second-best defenders.

The fit question is touches. Four stars need the ball, and Brown has to stay engaged when possessions do not start with him. If he embraces the two-way role, the Sixers may get a more efficient version of a player who just averaged almost 29.
