THE 4DK CONTEXT

THE RACE IS ONLY STARTING.

The 49ers have been the most complete early NFC team, but the schedule is about to test their health. The Vikings’ 5–0 start has been built on defensive volatility and timely contributions; their record deserves full respect. Kansas City’s 4–0 mark and pass-run balance keep the Chiefs among the conference favorites, while Jacksonville is proving its 4–1 start deserves more than a footnote.

At the other extreme, three 0–5 teams are staring at serious questions about health and their offensive identity. The standings capture outcomes; our power rankings and award watches are separate opinions about the strength of teams and individuals.
