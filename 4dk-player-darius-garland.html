The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Garland remains one of the league’s smoothest pick-and-roll guards. He can shoot behind screens, snake into the lane, play with pace and deliver passes from angles that keep a defense constantly rotating. The move to the Clippers gives him a new context after splitting last season between Cleveland and Los Angeles. At his best, he is a high-end offensive organizer. The limitations are size, defense and how much control he can maintain when playoff opponents switch bigger bodies onto him. Still, 6.7 assists with nearly 40 percent three-point shooting over the season keeps him comfortably in the Top 50.
