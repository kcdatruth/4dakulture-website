The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Sabonis remains an elite rebounder and one of the best passing centers in the league, but last season was interrupted after only 19 games. When he is right, Sacramento can run offense through his screening, dribble handoffs, touch around the rim and ability to grab a rebound and immediately ignite the next possession. The regular-season floor is extremely high. The hesitation comes from health and from the same postseason questions that have followed him for years: can he punish teams that sag off him, and can he survive every defensive matchup when opponents force him into space?
