Michael Vick gets remembered in flashes: the left-handed cannon, impossible escapes, Madden, Atlanta No. 7, the Philadelphia comeback. The Vick Files slows the story down.

What did Virginia Tech look like before he became a national phenomenon? What changed when the NFL tried to defend him? How much did the spectacle outgrow the actual production? What was lost? What was rebuilt? And how much of the modern quarterback conversation runs through Vick?

The archive has room for the electricity and the consequences. Both belong in the story.
