THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

J-Dub remains one of the cleanest winning-player profiles in the league. He can score efficiently, guard across positions, make quick decisions and create without stopping Oklahoma City’s ball movement. Last season was interrupted after 33 games, which is the biggest reason he sits this low despite already owning championship experience and All-NBA-level upside. His three-point percentage dipped to 29.9, but his strength, touch and secondary playmaking still translate in postseason basketball. A healthy season should push him back toward the 20s.
