The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Barnes affects too many areas of the game to evaluate him only by scoring average. He played 80 games last season, shot better than 50 percent from the field and gave Toronto 18.1 points, 7.5 rebounds and 5.9 assists while taking on major defensive responsibility. He can guard up and down the lineup, rebound and start the break, screen, pass from the elbows and attack smaller defenders inside.

Now Kawhi Leonard is back, which changes Scottie’s job in a useful way. He no longer has to be the answer to every difficult half-court possession. Kawhi can handle late-clock shot creation while Barnes becomes a connector, mismatch hunter and defensive weapon. That could make him more efficient and allow him to spend more energy on the parts of the game he does best.

The next leap still comes from shooting. At 30.4 percent from three, opponents will test Toronto’s spacing. If Barnes gets into the mid-30s while keeping the rest of his game intact, this ranking could look conservative.
