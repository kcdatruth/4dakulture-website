THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Haliburton did not play during the 2025–26 season while recovering from the Achilles injury that ended Indiana’s 2025 Finals run. That makes this ranking unusually difficult. His last healthy version was one of the league’s best offensive organizers: elite pace, advanced passing reads, deep shooting range and an ability to create advantages without monopolizing the ball.

The biggest question is not basketball IQ. It is physical trust. How quickly does the burst return? Can he plant, decelerate and change direction with the same confidence? Will defenses respect the pull-up three immediately, or will it take time for him to rebuild rhythm? Indiana spent an entire season learning how different its offense looks without him, and his return should restore the transition speed and early-clock creativity that made the Pacers so difficult to guard.

No. 24 is a hedge against a major injury, not a statement that 24 players are more talented. If Haliburton is physically himself by midseason, he can move back toward the Top 15.
