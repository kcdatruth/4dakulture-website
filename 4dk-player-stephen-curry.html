The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Steph’s gravity remains its own offensive system. He played only 43 games last season, which is the main reason he falls outside the Top 10, but when he was on the floor he still averaged 26.6 points while shooting 39.3 percent from three and 92.3 percent at the line. Defenses still pick him up 30 feet from the rim and still panic when he gives the ball up because everybody knows it may come back two seconds later.

Golden State’s challenge is preserving that version. Curry is 38, and the Warriors cannot ask him to sprint through endless off-ball actions every night and then expect the legs to be fresh in May. The roster has to create easier possessions for him.

Even at this age, there are few players you would rather have when a playoff defense is locked in and the game needs one shot. The ranking reflects availability and workload more than ability. A healthy Curry can still look like a Top-5 offensive player on any given night.
