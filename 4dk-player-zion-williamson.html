The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

The talent is nowhere near No. 44. The trust is. Zion played 62 games and shot 60 percent from the floor, which is a reminder that very few defenders can actually stop him from getting where he wants when he has momentum. He is still a paint-dominance machine with rare touch for his size. But availability, conditioning and the lack of a dependable perimeter counter have made every ranking of him a projection of what might happen rather than a reward for what consistently does happen. Another healthy season would push him much higher.
