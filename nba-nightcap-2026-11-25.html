GAME 09 / NBA CUP

116–111

BOX-SCORE SPOTLIGHT

The 4DK Breakdown

This is the signature game of the night. Wembanyama delivered another monstrous individual line, but Portland refused to let one transcendent performance decide the result. Avdija’s combination of scoring, rebounding and creation made him the offensive counterweight; Lillard’s shooting and passing added a second layer. What It Means
