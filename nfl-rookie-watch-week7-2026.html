WEEK 7 ROOKIE WATCH

Ten first-year players whose production, responsibility and development are shaping the 2026 season.

CUTOFF: Sunday, October 25, 2026. Rankings combine verified available production and explicitly labeled scouting judgments. Monday night is pending. Read the previous rookie board →
01
LB • New York Giants

Arvell Reese

A leading impact candidate because he is already being asked to do real defensive work. His season-to-date production includes 55 tackles, 1.5 sacks, five tackles for loss, an interception and four passes defended in the available Week 7 tracking. That blend of volume and versatility deserves top billing.

02
WR • Cleveland Browns

Denzel Boston

Boston has produced big plays rather than simply occupying a high-volume role. The available Week 7 tracking lists 20 receptions, 372 yards and three touchdowns. The Browns’ 27–24 win at Tennessee gives his production more relevance as Cleveland stays in the AFC North race.

03
LB • Washington Commanders

Sonny Styles

Styles has the range and down-to-down responsibility to be more than a highlight defender. His earlier 13-tackle, forced-fumble outing showed that he can influence high-leverage snaps. The next step is sustaining that playmaking across an entire month.

04
WR • Tennessee Titans

Carnell Tate

Tate’s 145-yard breakout in Week 4 remains a compelling ceiling indicator. His challenge is consistency in an offense that has struggled to protect leads and generate rhythm. A productive rookie receiver cannot control every target, but he can keep winning his routes.

05
LB • Miami Dolphins

Jacob Rodriguez

Miami has asked Rodriguez to handle meaningful defensive responsibility. His placement reflects the combination of active tackling and all-around involvement in available Week 7 rookie tracking; his trajectory is worth watching as Miami fights for traction.

06
DB • Dallas Cowboys

Caleb Downs

Downs has the instincts and coverage range to impact games without a gaudy stat line. Dallas still has its Monday night game against Philadelphia ahead, so this placement is provisional pending that performance.

07
RB • Arizona Cardinals

Jeremiyah Love

Love is a serious offensive rookie candidate because he offers explosiveness and receiving value. His opportunity is to turn flashes into consistent chain-moving production on a team still searching for its identity.

08
DB • Chicago Bears

Dillon Thieneman

Thieneman has emerged as a productive first-year defensive back. Chicago’s 30–24 Thursday victory over New England adds a useful team context; we will keep tracking coverage responsibility and impact rather than relying only on tackle totals.

09
QB • Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Jalon Daniels

The rookie quarterback’s opportunities make him one of the most important development stories in this class. The Buccaneers’ 43–13 win over Carolina is a team result; we are not assigning him unverified individual numbers. His growth will be measured by decisions, protections and repeatable passing efficiency.

10
EDGE • New York Jets

David Bailey

Bailey led our earlier board after a three-sack Week 5 performance. We still value that disruptive ceiling, but the Week 7 ranking puts more weight on repeated production and full-season involvement. He can rise again with another sustained run of pressure.
