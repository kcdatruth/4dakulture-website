The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Paolo already plays with the physicality and creation responsibility of a franchise scorer. At 6-foot-10 and 250 pounds, he can initiate from the perimeter, bully smaller defenders, rebound and make enough passing reads to function as an offensive hub. Last season’s 22.2 points, 8.4 rebounds and 5.2 assists show how complete the workload has become.

The next step is efficiency. A 30.5 percent three-point season still lets playoff defenses crowd his driving lanes, and Orlando’s half-court offense becomes much easier to guard when neither Paolo nor Franz is consistently bending the defense from deep. That does not mean Paolo needs to become a 40-percent shooter. He just has to become dangerous enough that defenders cannot live with going under.

Defensively, his size gives him versatility, but the best version of Paolo is the one who turns that physical advantage into impact every possession. If the jumper stabilizes and the turnovers come down, he has a real path from Top 20 to the edge of the Top 10.
