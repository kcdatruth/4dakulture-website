Week 9 gave the NFL another shakeup: a dominant Jacksonville opener, a packed Sunday slate and Buffalo's defensive closing act. All 15 games are final. Here's what each result means heading toward Week 10.

Original 4DK analysis; final scores verified against the NFL's official 2026 Week 9 schedule. This dated article is not a live standings or injury report.

Game 01 / 15

Jaguars vs. Ravens

Jaguars 33 — Ravens 10

Jacksonville made a forceful AFC statement. A 23-point road win over Baltimore strengthens its contender case, but the next step is reproducing that level against opponents that can force late-game adjustments.

Game 02 / 15

Bengals vs. Falcons

Bengals 34 — Falcons 17

Cincinnati's attack is building genuine momentum. Joe Burrow and his receivers controlled the matchup in Madrid, and the question is whether that offensive surge can carry the Bengals through the division race.

Game 03 / 15

Broncos vs. Panthers

Broncos 26 — Panthers 21

Denver survived a fourth-quarter deficit and found another way to win. That resilience is meaningful at 7–2, although the close finish shows why the Broncos still have to sharpen their consistency.

Game 04 / 15

Cowboys vs. Colts

Cowboys 27 — Colts 23

Dallas kept its season moving with a narrow road victory. The Cowboys can create explosive offense, but the margin leaves little room for mistakes as they chase the NFC playoff pack.

Game 05 / 15

Chiefs vs. Jets

Chiefs 35 — Jets 12

Kansas City handled business against New York with a convincing margin. The Chiefs remain a serious AFC threat; their challenge is turning this kind of command into a weekly standard against stronger defenses.

Game 06 / 15

Lions vs. Dolphins

Lions 19 — Dolphins 9

Detroit won a game that demanded patience rather than fireworks. Its defense held Miami to single digits, a useful reminder that a contender must have more than one route to victory.

Game 07 / 15

Saints vs. Browns

Saints 23 — Browns 22

New Orleans stole an overtime thriller by the thinnest margin. The Saints are still alive in a compressed NFC South, but surviving Cleveland is not the same as solving their offensive inconsistency.

Game 08 / 15

Giants vs. Eagles

Giants 19 — Eagles 11

New York's defense-first win deepened Philadelphia's troubles and strengthened the Giants' NFC East position. Protecting that advantage will require more sustained offensive production.

Game 09 / 15

Rams vs. Commanders

Rams 20 — Commanders 13

Los Angeles won a tight road game to remain relevant in the NFC West chase. The Rams' ability to finish close contests is valuable, but the division race offers little breathing room.

Game 10 / 15

Texans vs. Chargers

Texans 44 — Chargers 22

Houston produced one of the week's biggest scoring performances against winless Los Angeles. It is a reminder that the Texans' ceiling is higher than their record, provided the offense can sustain this efficiency.

Game 11 / 15

49ers vs. Raiders

49ers 38 — Raiders 17

San Francisco separated decisively in a rivalry matchup and kept pace with Buffalo atop the 4DK board. The 49ers have contender-level results; health and consistency will shape their stretch run.

Game 12 / 15

Packers vs. Patriots

Packers 45 — Patriots 27

Green Bay's offense erupted on the road, with Jordan Love leading the passing attack. The Packers showed why they cannot be discounted in a crowded NFC North race.

Game 13 / 15

Seahawks vs. Cardinals

Seahawks 17 — Cardinals 3

Seattle won with defense and control, holding Arizona to a field goal. That approach can travel, but the Seahawks must keep finding enough offense to separate from the NFC West pack.

Game 14 / 15

Bears vs. Buccaneers

Bears 27 — Buccaneers 24

Chicago found a way to win despite four Caleb Williams interceptions. The 7–2 record is impressive, but the giveaways are a warning against better playoff-caliber opposition.

Game 15 / 15

Bills vs. Vikings

Bills 23 — Vikings 6

Buffalo closed the week with a defensive statement in Minnesota. The Bills controlled the game and showed why their defense can be a difference-maker when the postseason margins tighten.
