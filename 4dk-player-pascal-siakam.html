The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Siakam remains one of the league’s most dependable two-way forwards. He gives Indiana size, transition offense, post scoring, playmaking and the ability to attack mismatches without needing to dominate the ball. His 24 points per game last season came while Haliburton missed the entire year, which forced Siakam into a larger creation burden than usual. The three-point shot was respectable at 35.8 percent, but his best work still comes from using length and footwork inside the arc. He may not be a first-option superstar, but his versatility gives a contender an answer to a lot of different problems.
