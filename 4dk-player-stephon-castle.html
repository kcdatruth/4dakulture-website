The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Castle’s second season confirmed that his Rookie of the Year campaign was only the beginning. He averaged 7.4 assists while continuing to defend with size and physicality, giving San Antonio another big initiator next to De’Aaron Fox and Wembanyama. The jumper is improving but remains the part of his game opponents will challenge most aggressively. What makes Castle different is that he can still help when the shot is not falling: he rebounds, defends, pushes pace and creates advantages with strength. If the pull-up shooting catches up to everything else, he has real Top-20 upside.
