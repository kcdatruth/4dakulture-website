4DK NFL NIGHTCAP · WEEK 7 · OCTOBER 22, 2026

CHICAGO HAS ITS QUARTERBACK.
NOW THE NFC NORTH HAS A PROBLEM.

BEARS 30 — PATRIOTS 24 · FINAL

Caleb Williams was cooking. Chicago's defense made the winning plays. And the Bears are starting to look like a team nobody in the NFC should overlook.

THE NIGHT IN NUMBERS

Caleb Williams: 308 passing yards, 2 passing touchdowns.
Drake Maye: 321 passing yards, 2 passing touchdowns, 1 interception, 1 rushing touchdown.
Luther Burden III: 4 receptions, 130 yards, 1 touchdown.

Caleb isn't just Chicago's future anymore

For years, the Bears have been searching for a quarterback who could consistently elevate the offense. Thursday was another reminder of what changes when that player finally arrives. Williams attacked downfield, created when the structure broke down and delivered in key situations against a New England team that had won three straight.

We're not putting him ahead of Patrick Mahomes, Josh Allen, Lamar Jackson, Jared Goff or Matthew Stafford just yet. But he's on the doorstep. The tools are already obvious, and his command of the offense is catching up with his talent.

Give Chicago's defense its flowers

Caleb will get the headlines, and deservedly so. But this wasn't a one-man win. Chicago's defense made timely plays, including a crucial end-zone interception of Maye. The Patriots moved the ball and kept the game close, so we won't pretend the Bears dominated every possession. What matters is that Chicago found ways to get stops when the stakes were highest.

That's complementary football. When the quarterback can put points on the board and the defense can change possessions, Chicago has a formula that travels.

Luther Burden III changes the equation

Four catches. 130 yards. A touchdown. Burden gave Williams a true explosive-play threat and punished New England for giving him opportunities. That kind of production forces defenses to account for more than Caleb's improvisation.

Maye showed promise, but the mistake was costly

Maye's 321 passing yards and three total touchdowns deserve respect. He gave the Patriots a chance and demonstrated why his own stock is rising. But his end-zone interception loomed large in a six-point loss. Against teams with playoff ambitions, promising drives must turn into points.

Now the NFC North has a real quarterback debate

Jared Goff is the more polished veteran with the longer résumé, but Caleb is already closing the gap and could challenge him for years. Jordan Love belongs in the same conversation as Williams right now. Minnesota's unbeaten start deserves respect, and Kyler has looked good, but we still want to see how that offense responds when adversity arrives.

That's the difference Chicago has found: certainty at the most important position. The Bears know what they're building around.

4DK Final Verdict

At 5–2, Chicago has earned its place in the NFC contender conversation. The Bears can challenge teams like San Francisco and Seattle, and there's a credible argument that they're already as dangerous as Green Bay. There are still questions to answer over a full season, but the days of automatically dismissing Chicago because of its quarterback situation are over.

4DK PLAYER OF THE GAME: Caleb Williams
4DK OFFENSIVE X-FACTOR: Luther Burden III
