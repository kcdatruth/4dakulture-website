MVP Watch

01 / MVP WATCH
Cincinnati Bengals · Nov 10 #7 · ▲ 6

Joe Burrow

Season snapshot
2,795 pass yds · 27 pass TD · 6 INT · 85 rush yds · 3 rush TD

Pittsburgh faced only seven Cincinnati third downs. Burrow completed 35 throws, four for scores, and never gave the Steelers an interception to shorten the field. We put him first because he can exploit the attention Chase draws twice on the same drive, first for a chunk gain and then for a touchdown to someone else. The test now is holding this efficiency when opponents make him complete twelve short passes to travel the same distance.

02 / MVP WATCH
San Francisco 49ers · Nov 10 #2 · — Hold

Brock Purdy

Season snapshot
2,369 pass yds · 20 pass TD · 5 INT · 222 rush yds · 2 rush TD

Dallas made the 49ers score almost every time they had the ball. Purdy responded with 389 yards on 28 attempts and no picks. McCaffrey supplied an extraordinary share of those yards after getting the ball, but finding the back in space is precisely the choice that keeps this offense dangerous. We keep Purdy second because San Francisco needed both the explosive decision and the clean possession to escape.

03 / MVP WATCH
Buffalo Bills · Nov 10 #1 · ▼ 2

Josh Allen

Season snapshot
2,223 pass yds · 13 pass TD · 7 INT · 290 rush yds · 11 rush TD

Two rushing touchdowns came out of a five-yard rushing afternoon. Allen's value to Buffalo lives in that contrast. Defenses have to account for him at the goal line even on a day Cook owns the open field. An 11-for-21 passing line and an interception leave room for Burrow and Purdy above him. Eleven rushing scores through nine games make it hard to put anybody else ahead of him.

04 / MVP WATCH
Detroit Lions · Nov 10 #3 · ▼ 1

Jared Goff

Season snapshot
2,437 pass yds · 18 pass TD · 4 INT · 23 rush yds · 1 rush TD

Amon-Ra St. Brown needed just four catches for 98 yards and two scores, while Gibbs produced almost the same receiving yardage on seven. Goff can hand the defense two different assignment problems from one formation. Two interceptions in Germany keep us from treating 303 yards as a flawless performance. His season total of four picks puts the afternoon in perspective and keeps him fourth.

05 / MVP WATCH
Seattle Seahawks · Nov 10 #5 · — Hold

Jaxon Smith-Njigba

Season snapshot
79 rec · 1,207 rec yds · 10 rec TD

Drew Lock threw for 240 yards against Las Vegas. Smith-Njigba supplied 171 of them. A 71-yard gain will bend any box score, but the other seven catches still totaled a hundred. That is the strongest part of his case to us. Seattle can ask him for field position, an explosive finish and an answer for a different quarterback, all in the same game.

06 / MVP WATCH
Kansas City Chiefs · Nov 10 #6 · — Hold

Patrick Mahomes

Season snapshot
2,385 pass yds · 19 pass TD · 4 INT · 100 rush yds · 1 rush TD

Five touchdown throws on 35 attempts explain the score in Atlanta. What keeps Mahomes in the race is how little the defense gains by taking away the obvious target. Rashee Rice led Kansas City in receiving yards, yet the rookie back caught two scores. We'd still ask whether this offense can convert more third downs when the big play disappears. A seven-win start gives Mahomes time to answer.

07 / MVP WATCH
Jacksonville Jaguars · Nov 10 #4 · ▼ 3

Trevor Lawrence

Season snapshot
2,284 pass yds · 21 pass TD · 5 INT · 106 rush yds · 0 rush TD

Lawrence hit Parker Washington for 152 yards and still had another 200 passing yards to distribute. That matters more to us than simply running up a total on Tennessee. Tilting coverage toward the first explosive target doesn't solve the rest of the field. Twenty-one passing scores against five interceptions keep his full-season case strong. His drop reflects the competition for places on this board.

08 / MVP WATCH
Detroit Lions · New to Top 10

Jahmyr Gibbs

Season snapshot
41 rec · 384 rec yds · 2 rec TD · 1,000 rush yds · 11 rush TD

His 252 yards from scrimmage divided almost neatly into the jobs of a lead runner and a starting receiver. A carry can bring a safety forward on one series, and a catch can punish that same alignment on the next. We give Gibbs his own ballot spot alongside Goff because Detroit can change the question it asks a defense without changing the player who touches the ball.

09 / MVP WATCH
Green Bay Packers · Nov 10 #8 · ▼ 1

Jordan Love

Season snapshot
2,838 pass yds · 20 pass TD · 7 INT · 23 rush yds · 2 rush TD

Seventeen completions brought 220 yards against Minnesota. Green Bay still scored ten points because productive throws were interrupted by losses behind the line and four team giveaways. We separate Love's one interception from that team total. His season production keeps him on the board. For him to climb, we'd want more possessions where an early completion buys the offense an easier down and an actual scoring chance.

10 / MVP WATCH
Buffalo Bills · Nov 10 #10 · — Hold

James Cook

Season snapshot
12 rec · 110 rec yds · 0 rec TD · 971 rush yds · 9 rush TD

Even if you take the 74-yard run out of his afternoon, Cook gained 83 on his other seventeen carries. That is the argument for keeping him here despite the attention Allen commands near the goal line. Buffalo can ride the back for ordinary gains and still make a missed tackle cost an opponent most of the field. His 971 rushing yards make the run threat credible before Allen decides anything.
