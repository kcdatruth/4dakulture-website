This is deliberately different from our MVP Watch. Quarterbacks are not getting an automatic advantage because their position is more valuable. An elite tackle, receiver, edge rusher, corner or running back can rank above a quarterback if we believe he is playing better football right now.

THE 4DK FORMULA

2026 HAS TO MATTER.

Current production carries the most weight. Availability is a major factor. Film-level impact beyond the box score matters too. Then we use 2025 production and established star level to stop three games from completely rewriting what we already know about a player.
