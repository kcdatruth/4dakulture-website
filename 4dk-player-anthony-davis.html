The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Pure talent says Davis should be much higher. Availability is why he is not. He appeared in only 20 games last season before another year ended with injury, and that changes how much confidence you can place in him entering a new season. When healthy, the basketball case is easy: elite rim protection, switchability, rebounding, lob finishing, touch around the basket and enough face-up skill to punish slower bigs. Washington is not asking him to become a different player. It is asking him to stay on the floor long enough for those strengths to matter over an entire season. A healthy AD is still capable of playing like a Top-15 player.
