The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Fox no longer has to carry every possession to be valuable. In San Antonio, his speed gives the Spurs a different kind of pressure next to Wembanyama and Stephon Castle. He can collapse a defense before it gets set, get downhill late in the clock and create the kind of easy paint touches that open the floor for everyone else. Last season’s scoring dipped, but the efficiency stayed solid and the fit around him is better suited to winning. The next step is showing that his pace and clutch creation can translate consistently when the Spurs are playing meaningful postseason basketball.
