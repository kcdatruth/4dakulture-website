The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Maxey has become a genuine star, not just the fast guard who benefited from playing next to Embiid. He averaged 28.3 points and 6.6 assists last season, shot 36.7 percent from three and continued to pressure the rim at a pace few defenders can match. The speed is the headline, but the control is what changed his ceiling.

Philadelphia’s new roster may lower his raw scoring volume, but it could raise his efficiency and postseason impact. LeBron and Jaylen Brown give him more secondary creators, while Embiid still commands extra defenders. Maxey should see more possessions where the defense is already tilted before he attacks.

The challenge is balancing aggression with playmaking. On a roster this talented, the point guard cannot turn every opening into a scoring race. He has to know when to push, when to organize and when to let the other stars control the possession. If he masters that, his impact can exceed the box score even if his points drop.
