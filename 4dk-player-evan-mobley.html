The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Mobley’s defensive impact still puts him in rare company. He can protect the rim, switch onto guards, cover huge amounts of space and erase mistakes behind aggressive perimeter defenders. The offensive game continues to expand, but the three-point shot fell back under 30 percent and the free-throw shooting was a concern. Cleveland does not need him to become a 25-point scorer; it needs him to punish smaller matchups, make quick reads and keep defenses honest outside the paint. His two-way ceiling remains much higher than No. 30, but the offensive consistency has to catch up to the defense.
