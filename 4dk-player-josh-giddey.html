The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Giddey starts the list because his size and playmaking make him one of the league’s most unusual lead guards. At 6-foot-7, he can rebound the ball and start the break himself, see over smaller defenders and create offense without needing to win every possession as a scorer. The 9.1 assists per game last season show how much responsibility he can handle as an organizer. The questions are still the same ones that have followed him: how much defenses respect the jumper in a playoff setting and whether he can hold up defensively when opponents hunt matchups. Chicago gives him the runway to keep proving he is more than a box-score accumulator.
