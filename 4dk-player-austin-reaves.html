The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Reaves has grown from a complementary guard into a legitimate high-level creator. Twenty-three points and 5.5 assists on 49 percent shooting is not role-player production, and the Lakers increasingly trust him to run offense when Luka sits or when defenses overload toward the superstar. He changes pace well, draws fouls, attacks closeouts and has become much more comfortable creating from ball screens. The question now is whether the defense and shot creation hold at the same level against the best playoff teams. If they do, the Luka–Reaves partnership gives Los Angeles one of the league’s strongest offensive backcourts.
