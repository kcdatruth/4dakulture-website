4DK NFL • WEEK 1 WATCH GUIDE

13 GAMES.
ONE FULL SUNDAY.

The first full Sunday of the 2026 season gives us an AFC heavyweight fight, two loaded NFC division games, a Vegas opener with a new quarterback, and a Sunday-night rivalry to close it out.

By Kcdatruth • 4 Da Kulture • September 13, 2026
Early Window8 games • 1:00 ET
Late Window4 games • 4:25 ET
Sunday NightDAL @ NYG
4DK Game of the DayBUF @ HOU

Week 1 is where expectations finally have to survive contact. The offseason is over. New coaches, new quarterbacks and all those “on paper” arguments get their first real test. Buffalo at Houston is the headline, but the late window is loaded too: Green Bay–Minnesota, Washington–Philadelphia and Miami–Las Vegas all come with bigger questions than a normal opener.

Here’s the 4DK watch guide — every Sunday matchup, the angle that matters and what could decide it.

1:00 ET • The Early Window

8 Games
FOX • Charlotte
CHI @ CAR

Caleb Williams and Ben Johnson begin Year 2 together against a Carolina defense that spent to get faster and more disruptive. Bryce Young’s next step is the other half of the story.

4DK Watch: Can Carolina pressure Caleb without exposing the back end?

Deeper read: Chicago wants to live in rhythm with Caleb Williams, but Carolina’s biggest path to an upset is making him hold the ball. Jaelan Phillips and Devin Lloyd give the Panthers more speed in the front seven, while Bryce Young has to punish a Bears defense that gave up too many yards last year. If this turns into a clean-pocket shootout, Chicago has more answers; if it gets muddy, Carolina can drag it into the fourth quarter.

Players to Watch

Caleb Williams • Bryce Young • Jaelan Phillips

Injury Report

CHI: Rome Odunze (calf), Ozzy Trapilo (knee), Xavier Woods (groin) questionable. CAR: Patrick Jones II (back) out.

CBS • Indianapolis
BAL @ IND

Lamar Jackson opens under a new Ravens staff after an injury-disrupted 2025. Indianapolis showed last season that its offense can score when Daniel Jones is healthy.

4DK Watch: Baltimore’s new defensive identity vs. Indy tempo.

Deeper read: Baltimore’s opener is a test of how quickly Jesse Minter’s defense can take shape and whether Lamar Jackson immediately looks like himself again. Indianapolis can score when Daniel Jones is protected, but the Colts do not want to live in obvious passing downs against Baltimore. The first team to control early downs should control the game.

Players to Watch

Lamar Jackson • Zay Flowers • Daniel Jones

Injury Report

BAL: Nnamdi Madubuike (neck) and Teddye Buchanan (knee) out; Devontez Walker (groin) questionable. IND: Austin Ajiake (hamstring) out.

FOX • Pittsburgh
ATL @ PIT

Atlanta’s quarterback plan changed late in the week with Tua Tagovailoa ruled out, putting Cooper Rush in the opener. Pittsburgh gets a home game to establish its veteran offense early.

4DK Watch: Can Atlanta survive obvious passing downs?

Deeper read: Atlanta’s entire script changes with Cooper Rush starting. The Falcons need manageable third downs, field position and a run game that keeps Pittsburgh from teeing off. The Steelers can be patient: Aaron Rodgers does not need to chase explosives if the defense keeps handing him short fields.

Players to Watch

Cooper Rush • Aaron Rodgers • DK Metcalf

Injury Report

ATL: Tua Tagovailoa (oblique), Michael Penix Jr. (knee), Cameron Williams (ankle) and Billy Bowman Jr. (Achilles) out; Za’Darius Smith (shoulder) questionable. PIT: Joey Porter Jr. (back) questionable.

CBS • Jacksonville
CLE @ JAX

Jacksonville enters after a 13-win season with Trevor Lawrence in Year 2 under Liam Coen. Cleveland starts Deshaun Watson and opens a season without Myles Garrett anchoring its defense.

4DK Watch: Can Cleveland keep this ugly enough to stay close?

Deeper read: Jacksonville has the cleaner identity: Trevor Lawrence in Year 2 of Liam Coen’s system against a Cleveland roster trying to replace star power on defense and manufacture offense around Deshaun Watson. Cleveland’s best chance is to shorten the game, steal a turnover and keep Lawrence from getting comfortable on early downs.

Players to Watch

Trevor Lawrence • Deshaun Watson • KC Concepcion

Injury Report

CLE: Teven Jenkins (back) out; Parker Brailsford (thumb) questionable. JAX: LeQuint Allen Jr. (hip) questionable.

FOX • Cincinnati
TB @ CIN

Joe Burrow is healthy, Ja’Marr Chase and Tee Higgins are intact, and Cincinnati looks built to start fast. Tampa Bay is reworking the passing game in the post-Mike Evans era.

4DK Watch: Potential shootout if Tampa protects long enough.

Deeper read: This has real shootout potential. Cincinnati’s advantage is continuity — Joe Burrow with Ja’Marr Chase and Tee Higgins — while Tampa is still defining its post-Mike Evans passing game. If Baker Mayfield gets time, the Bucs can trade scores. If Cincinnati’s rebuilt front gets home, the Bengals can separate.

Players to Watch

Joe Burrow • Ja’Marr Chase • Emeka Egbuka

Injury Report

TB: Jalen McMillan (knee), Sean Tucker (hamstring) and Justin Skule (oblique) doubtful; Christian Rozeboom (foot) questionable. CIN: Shemar Stewart (knee) doubtful.

CBS • Nashville
NYJ @ TEN

Robert Saleh opens his Titans tenure against his former team. Tennessee is still building around Cam Ward, while New York enters with a veteran roster and Geno Smith running the offense.

4DK Watch: Which offensive line owns the game?

Deeper read: This feels like a line-of-scrimmage game. Tennessee can make Geno Smith uncomfortable with Jeffery Simmons, Jermaine Johnson II and John Franklin-Myers, but New York owns the experience edge on offense. Cam Ward does not need to win the game by himself; he needs to avoid the one or two rookie mistakes that flip field position.

Players to Watch

Geno Smith • Cam Ward • Jeffery Simmons

Injury Report

NYJ: Tim Patrick (groin) and Joseph Ossai (foot) out; Kene Nwangwu (back) and D’Angelo Ponds (calf) doubtful. TEN: Cedric Gray (concussion) out.

FOX • Detroit
NO @ DET

Detroit wants a reset after a disappointing 2025, and Ford Field remains one of the league’s toughest places to chase points. New Orleans counters with second-year quarterback Tyler Shough.

4DK Watch: Lions playmakers vs. Saints defensive front.

Deeper read: Detroit should want tempo and points; New Orleans should want possessions shortened. Jared Goff is at his best indoors, and the Lions have enough skill talent to force Tyler Shough into a chase game. The Saints’ chance is Alvin Kamara controlling pace and their front creating pressure without blitzing.

Players to Watch

Jared Goff • Tyler Shough • Alvin Kamara

Injury Report

NO: Cameron Jordan (hamstring), Oscar Delp (hamstring) and Audric Estimé (thigh) out; Alvin Kamara (knee) questionable. DET: Christian Izien (groin) questionable.

CBS • Houston
BUF @ HOU

This is the early-window headliner: Josh Allen, James Cook III and new WR1 DJ Moore against a Houston defense loaded at every level. It feels like January football arriving in September.

4DK Watch: Buffalo protection vs. Houston’s rush.

Deeper read: This is the chess match of the early window. Houston has already shown it can make Josh Allen uncomfortable by compressing the pocket and forcing him into late-down hero ball. Buffalo counters with the league’s reigning rushing king in James Cook III, a new WR1 in DJ Moore and an offensive line that can keep the offense on schedule. If Buffalo stays out of third-and-long, the Bills can flip the matchup that has bothered them the last two seasons.

Players to Watch

Josh Allen • James Cook III • Will Anderson Jr.

Injury Report

BUF: Ty Johnson (hamstring), Jordan Hancock (quad) and T.J. Sanders (knee) questionable. HOU: No official game-status designations listed.

4:25 ET • The Late Window

4 Games
CBS • Los Angeles
ARI @ LAC

Justin Herbert gets his first regular-season look in Mike McDaniel’s offense. Arizona starts a new era under Mike LaFleur and faces a tough opening assignment on the road.

4DK Watch: How fast does the Chargers offense look?

Deeper read: The Chargers want to prove the Mike McDaniel offense can turn Justin Herbert’s arm talent into easy explosives. Arizona’s counter is a young, fast pass rush led by Josh Sweat and Walter Nolen. The key is whether the Cardinals can make Herbert move off his first read before Los Angeles’ speed stretches the field.

Players to Watch

Justin Herbert • Trey McBride • Josh Sweat

Injury Report

ARI: Isaiah Adams (knee), Dadrion Taylor-Demerson (ribs) and Garrett Williams (Achilles) out; Jeremiyah Love (ankle) questionable. LAC: Deane Leonard (abdomen) out; Tuli Tuipulotu (hip) questionable.

CBS • Minnesota
GB @ MIN

Division game, new quarterback story in Minnesota, and Green Bay enters without two major pieces in Micah Parsons and Josh Jacobs. Jordan Love may have to carry more of the offense.

4DK Watch: Brian Flores making Green Bay one-dimensional.

Deeper read: Green Bay enters short-handed and that changes the math. Without Micah Parsons and Josh Jacobs, Jordan Love has to carry more of the offense while Brian Flores can attack a thinner backfield. Minnesota’s new variable is Kyler Murray: if his mobility stresses Green Bay horizontally and Justin Jefferson wins outside, the Vikings can dictate the game instead of reacting to it.

Players to Watch

Jordan Love • Kyler Murray • Justin Jefferson

Injury Report

GB: Warren Brinson (calf) out; Aaron Banks (knee), Ty’Ron Hopper (Achilles) and Zach Bako-Bewele (knee) questionable. MIN: No official game-status designations listed on the final report.

FOX • Las Vegas
MIA @ LV

Miami begins the Malik Willis era against a Raiders team handing the offense to Kirk Cousins. Ashton Jeanty is expected to play, while Brock Bowers is expected to miss time after a meniscus procedure.

4DK Watch: Can Miami make Cousins play from behind?

Deeper read: Miami wants speed and space; Las Vegas wants to make Malik Willis play quarterback from the pocket. Maxx Crosby is the problem Miami has to solve, while De’Von Achane gives the Dolphins a way to punish overaggressive fronts. For the Raiders, Kirk Cousins does not need to be spectacular — he needs to keep Ashton Jeanty involved and avoid putting the defense back on the field too quickly.

Players to Watch

Malik Willis • De’Von Achane • Maxx Crosby / Ashton Jeanty

Injury Report

MIA: No official game-status designations listed. LV: Brock Bowers (knee) out; Ashton Jeanty practiced in full and carries no game-status designation.

FOX • Philadelphia
WAS @ PHI

Jayden Daniels gets a brutal first test against a Philadelphia defense built around pressure inside and speed behind it. Washington’s rebuilt defense gets an equally immediate stress test.

4DK Watch: Can Washington protect Daniels long enough to create explosives?

Deeper read: Washington rebuilt its defense, but this game is really about protecting Jayden Daniels. Philadelphia can collapse the middle with Jalen Carter and Jordan Davis, then close passing windows with Quinyon Mitchell and Cooper DeJean. If Daniels is constantly escaping before routes develop, Washington will struggle to create the explosive plays it needs.

Players to Watch

Jayden Daniels • Jalen Carter • Quinyon Mitchell

Injury Report

WAS: No official game-status designations listed. PHI: Jonathan Greenard (pectoral) and Eli Stowers (hamstring/quad) out.

Sunday Night Football

8:20 ET • NBC
MetLife Stadium • NFC East
DAL @ NYG

Dallas and New York close Sunday with a rivalry game carrying fresh energy. The Giants enter under John Harbaugh with Cam Skattebo healthy, Odell Beckham back and Malik Nabers pushing toward a return.

4DK Watch: Can New York’s pass rush turn this into a fourth-quarter game?

Deeper read: The Giants want this to feel like a new era immediately: John Harbaugh, Jaxson Dart, a healthy Cam Skattebo and a deeper pass rush. Dallas still has the steadier quarterback situation, but its offensive line is already being tested without Tyler Smith. If New York can win up front and keep Dak Prescott from living in rhythm, this can become exactly the kind of ugly prime-time rivalry game the Giants want.

Players to Watch

Dak Prescott • Jaxson Dart • Malik Nabers

Injury Report

DAL: Malik Davis (hip) out; Pro Bowl LG Tyler Smith is on injured reserve. NYG: Malik Nabers (knee) questionable.
