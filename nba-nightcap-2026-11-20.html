Harden Makes History. The Cup Gets Serious.

GAME 01 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Atlanta Hawks 111 — Charlotte Hornets 97

Atlanta’s defensive pressure flips the game

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Jalen Johnson: 26 points; Aaron Wiggins: 19 points. Charlotte: Moussa Diabaté 21 points, 15 rebounds; Coby White 9 assists.

THE TURNING POINT

Charlotte built a 14-point second-quarter lead, but Atlanta won the third 31–19.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

The Hawks did not win this with a clean opening stretch. Their answer was to make the Hornets’ possessions more uncomfortable, convert mistakes into transition chances and trust Johnson as the offensive anchor. Johnson and Dyson Daniels each recorded four steals; that activity matters because Atlanta can create offense without needing every possession to start with a perfect half-court set. The Hawks generated 36 points off turnovers. Charlotte’s early advantage shows it can find productive offense, but the third-quarter reversal exposes how quickly those gains disappear when ball security collapses. Atlanta finishes 3–1 in East Group C; Charlotte is eliminated.

GAME 02 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Orlando Magic 113 — Brooklyn Nets 111

Franz Wagner supplies the shot Orlando needed

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Franz Wagner: 35 points, 7 assists. Brooklyn: Julius Randle 27 points, 6 assists; Day’Ron Sharpe 11 rebounds.

THE TURNING POINT

Wagner hit the go-ahead jumper in the closing moments.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

Orlando’s offense can look crowded when opponents take away the first driving lane, but Wagner’s combination of size, patience and shotmaking gives it a release valve. This was not simply a high-volume scoring night: seven assists show that his threat as a scorer created opportunities for others. The decisive jumper reinforced his value as a late-clock option. Brooklyn competed for four quarters and forced the Magic to execute rather than coast, but competitive losses still leave the Nets searching for a reliable closing formula. Orlando moves to 3–0 in East Group A, strengthening its position in the Cup race.

GAME 03 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Cleveland Cavaliers 128 — Indiana Pacers 119

James Harden turns a Cup game into history

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

James Harden: 51 points, 6 rebounds, 6 assists, 7 made threes. Indiana: Ivica Zubac 21 points, 10 rebounds; Andrew Nembhard 8 assists.

THE TURNING POINT

Harden had already scored 40 by the end of the third quarter.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

The Cavaliers got the full Harden scoring package: deep-range punishment, manipulation of defensive positioning and the ability to dictate when the game sped up. The most important team question is whether Cleveland can keep its offense balanced when one creator becomes this dominant. Against Indiana, the answer was clear enough for a nine-point win, aided by Cleveland’s 43.2-percent three-point shooting. Indiana had to choose between conceding Harden’s individual scoring and risking open looks elsewhere. Harden’s 26th career 50-point game moved him past Kobe Bryant for third on the all-time list, and Cleveland improved to 2–1 in East Group B. This is our 4DK Player of the Night performance.

GAME 04 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Toronto Raptors 105 — Detroit Pistons 102

Scottie Barnes refuses to let Toronto’s skid define it

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Scottie Barnes: 29 points; Allen Graves: 20 points, 10 rebounds; Immanuel Quickley: 10 assists. Detroit: Cade Cunningham 23 points, 9 assists.

THE TURNING POINT

After Cunningham put Detroit ahead 102–101, Barnes answered with the decisive basket and Toronto forced a turnover.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

This was the type of ugly, demanding win Toronto needed after six straight losses. Barnes did more than lead the scoring column: he took responsibility for the possession that decided the game. Graves gave the Raptors a second source of energy in his first NBA start, and his rebounding mattered in a contest that turned on second chances. Detroit’s comeback from 14 down demonstrated its resilience, but the late turnover and missed opportunity to force overtime undercut the rally. Toronto’s result does not erase the issues that produced its skid, yet it offers a tangible model for competing while short-handed: defend, rebound and let Barnes organize the final possessions.

GAME 05 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Washington Wizards 98 — Chicago Bulls 94

Anthony Davis and AJ Dybantsa give Washington two timelines at once

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Anthony Davis: 36 points, 11 rebounds; AJ Dybantsa: 20 points, 10 rebounds, 6 assists. Chicago: Norman Powell 16 points; Josh Giddey 7 assists.

THE TURNING POINT

Washington seized control with a 23–9 third quarter and survived Chicago’s late push.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

The Wizards’ ceiling looks different when Davis supplies veteran interior dominance and Dybantsa contributes across the box score rather than chasing points alone. Davis was the dependable scoring foundation; the rookie’s rebounding and six assists hinted at an offensive role broader than a pure wing finisher. Dybantsa became the first Washington teenager with consecutive 20-point, 10-rebound games. Chicago struggled to generate enough offense in the third, then failed to complete the comeback despite making the closing minutes tense. Washington reaches 2–1 in East Group C. The longer-term question is whether its young talent and veteran core can turn these Cup flashes into consistent execution.

GAME 06 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Oklahoma City Thunder 126 — New Orleans Pelicans 120

Shai’s closing command meets Aday Mara’s breakout

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander: 40 points; Aday Mara: 22 points, 16 rebounds; Jared McCain: 18 points. New Orleans: Zion Williamson and Jeremiah Fears 22 points each; Zion 7 assists.

THE TURNING POINT

Oklahoma City overcame a 16-point third-quarter deficit and won the fourth 34–22.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

The Thunder needed every part of their two-speed attack. Gilgeous-Alexander delivered 21 of his 40 after halftime, repeatedly restoring control when New Orleans threatened to hold its advantage. Mara gave Oklahoma City a different form of pressure: vertical finishing, offensive presence and 16 rebounds that helped stabilize possessions. His eight dunks placed him in rare rookie company. The Pelicans’ 120 points show that they created meaningful offense, but their fourth-quarter defense could not hold up against Shai’s shotmaking and OKC’s interior support. Oklahoma City moves to 2–1 in West Group B while New Orleans is eliminated.

GAME 07 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Phoenix Suns 111 — Utah Jazz 101

Booker steadies Phoenix while Peterson keeps building

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Devin Booker: 23 points; Mikal Bridges 9 rebounds. Utah: Darryn Peterson 20 points, 10 rebounds, 8 assists; Jaxson Hayes 14 rebounds.

THE TURNING POINT

Phoenix established enough separation to finish with a double-digit victory.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

The Suns did not need an overwhelming scoring explosion from Booker to take care of business. Utah closed to four with a 9–0 run, but Phoenix scored the final six points. His 23 points anchored a measured offensive performance, and Phoenix avoided letting Utah’s young creators turn the game into a late-possession coin flip. The Jazz have a significant development story in Peterson, whose near triple-double shows how many ways he can influence a game even when the team loses. Utah’s challenge is turning that individual creation into more consistent defensive stops and a reliable supporting offense. Phoenix moves to 1–1 in West Group A; Utah drops to 0–3 and is eliminated from Cup advancement.

GAME 08 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Denver Nuggets 133 — Houston Rockets 124

Denver wins the duel of big-man playmakers

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Nikola Jokić: 26 points, 7 rebounds, 11 assists; Christian Braun and Cameron Johnson: 19 points each. Houston: Alperen Şengün 28 points, 11 rebounds, 10 assists; Fred VanVleet 25 points.

THE TURNING POINT

Denver closed on a 23–9 run after a game featuring 32 lead changes.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

This was a matchup of offenses that can attack from every angle. Şengün’s triple-double gave Houston a hub in the middle of the floor, while VanVleet’s 25 points kept pressure on Denver’s perimeter defense. But Jokić’s passing made the Nuggets’ supporting pieces dangerous, and Braun’s efficient finishing and defensive activity were crucial complements. The deciding stretch was not a single spectacular shot: Denver finally strung together enough stops to make its late offense count. The Nuggets move to 2–0 in West Group A, while Houston falls to 1–2. For 4DK, the key distinction is late-game cohesion rather than which center posted the flashier stat line.

GAME 09 · NBA CUP · FINAL

Los Angeles Lakers 106 — Sacramento Kings 104

Lakers escape, but Acuff’s rise demands attention

BOX SCORE SPOTLIGHT

Lakers: Luka Dončić 25 PTS, 8 AST; Matisse Thybulle 14 PTS (4-for-5 3PT); Quentin Grimes 13 PTS. Kings: De’Andre Hunter 30 PTS; Domantas Sabonis 19 PTS, 16 REB; Darius Acuff Jr. 18 PTS, 12 AST.

THE TURNING POINT

Quentin Grimes hit the go-ahead three off Matisse Thybulle’s pass with 57 seconds remaining.

WHAT WE LEARNED — THE 4DK VERDICT

A narrow home win over Sacramento counts in the standings, but it should also prompt scrutiny of Los Angeles’ offensive execution. The Kings made the Lakers work through the final stretch, and the margin suggests there is still room to sharpen closing possessions. Dončić’s 25 points and eight assists reinforced how much he shapes the Lakers’ shot creation, but Thybulle’s four threes and late pass to Grimes made the difference when Sacramento threatened to steal the game. Sacramento’s bigger development is Acuff, whose 18 points and 12 assists showed advanced table-setting even as his 8-for-23 shooting revealed the efficiency challenge ahead. The Lakers move to 2–1 in West Group C, while the Kings fall to 0–3. This result is a win for Los Angeles, but not a reason to ignore the work ahead.

4DK PLAYER OF THE NIGHT

James Harden · Cleveland Cavaliers

51 points, 6 rebounds, 6 assists, seven three-pointers. We recognize Harden over Shai Gilgeous-Alexander’s 40 and Franz Wagner’s game-winner because his performance delivered both a Cup victory and a historic individual milestone: his 26th career 50-point game moved him past Kobe Bryant into third all-time. He did it with Cleveland needing a win against a familiar Eastern opponent. Honorable mentions: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Franz Wagner, Anthony Davis and Aday Mara.
