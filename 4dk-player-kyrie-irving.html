THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

This ranking is almost entirely about availability, not basketball skill. Kyrie missed the entire 2025–26 season while recovering from ACL surgery, so there is no honest way to rank him as if the layoff did not happen. But the talent never belonged in the 40s. Healthy Kyrie is still one of the most complete one-on-one scorers alive: elite handle, either-hand finishing, pull-up shooting, footwork and the ability to make impossible late-clock possessions look routine. The question entering 2026–27 is how much of that burst and change of direction returns after such a long absence. If his body cooperates, this ranking can become outdated fast.
