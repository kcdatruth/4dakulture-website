The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Holmgren’s two-way value is already elite enough to make a ranking this low feel harsh. He protects the rim, moves his feet on the perimeter, spaces to three and finishes efficiently without needing plays called for him. Last season he shot 55.7 percent from the field and 36.2 percent from three while anchoring one of the league’s best defenses. His frame will always create questions against the strongest post scorers, but Oklahoma City’s scheme rarely leaves him alone in those matchups. The next jump comes from more self-created offense. If Chet becomes someone who can punish switches and generate points late in the clock, he will move well inside the Top 20.
