THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Wagner does almost everything you want from a modern wing. He can initiate offense, attack smaller defenders in the post, beat bigger players off the bounce and guard multiple positions. Last season was limited to 34 games, but he still averaged 20.6 points on 48 percent shooting. The three-point shot finally moved in the right direction at 34.5 percent after being the obvious weakness in his profile. Orlando’s offensive ceiling depends heavily on whether Wagner and Paolo can coexist as big creators without shrinking the floor. If Franz stays healthy and the jumper holds, No. 33 could look low.
