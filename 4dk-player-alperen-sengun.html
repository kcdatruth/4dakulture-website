The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Şengün is one of the league’s most creative offensive bigs. He can score with either hand in the post, play through contact, hit cutters from the elbow and function as a hub when Houston wants to run offense through the middle of the floor. The 20.4 points, 8.9 rebounds and 6.2 assists last season show how much he can touch every possession. The ranking holds him outside the top tier because defense still determines how high a center can climb in playoff basketball. If he becomes more dependable protecting space and the rim, his offense is already good enough for a major jump.
