The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Booker remains one of the cleanest three-level scorers in the league. He averaged 26.1 points and six assists last season even as Phoenix continued changing the roster around him. His midrange game is still elite, the pull-up three demands respect and he has become a much better passer than the pure scorer label suggests.

What keeps him outside the Top 20 is less about a weakness and more about total control. The players above him either drive elite offense every possession, offer greater two-way impact or have shown they can bend a playoff series in more ways. Booker can absolutely be the best scorer in a series, but when defenses blitz him and force the ball out, Phoenix needs him to punish those coverages as a playmaker just as consistently.

The Suns are his team now. That clarity may help. If Booker combines 27-plus scoring with efficient playmaking and keeps the offense stable regardless of lineup, the ranking will rise.
