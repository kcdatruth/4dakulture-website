4DK ORIGINAL ANALYSIS · NOVEMBER 17, 2026

ROOKIE WATCH TOP 10

Cam Boozer takes the throne. Yaxel remains right behind him. Acuff holds third, and Brayden Burries claims the final spot after a three-way debate. This is the 4DK collective's editorial ranking, not an official NBA award ballot.

#1 Boozer#2 Yaxel#3 Acuff#10 BurriesOn the bubble
01

Cameron Boozer

Memphis is winning and Boozer's passing, rebounding and feel make him more than a scoring prospect. The 4DK argument for No. 1 is how many different possessions he can improve without demanding the ball. His next test is maintaining that influence when opponents make him finish through contact.

02

Yaxel Lendeborg

Lendeborg remains a dynamic two-way contributor. Golden State needs his versatility, especially when its perimeter creation is strained. A recent cold shooting stretch matters, but we will not reduce his case to a few misses: the rebounding, decisions and defensive assignments all count.

03

Darius Acuff Jr.

Acuff's shot creation and playmaking stand out even while Sacramento struggles. High usage is both opportunity and burden. We want to see him balance attacking for his own offense with generating cleaner looks for teammates and cutting down costly possessions.

04

Darryn Peterson

Peterson's ability to manufacture a look gives him a strong top-four case. His climb is an editorial projection of his impact and ceiling, not a declaration that he has already outproduced every player below him. Shot selection and availability will determine whether he stays here.

05

AJ Dybantsa

Dybantsa rises into the top five on talent and the value of his two-way potential. We are keeping availability central to the evaluation: flashes alone cannot outweigh a sustained body of work indefinitely. His decision-making against loaded defenses is the next measuring stick.

06

Caleb Wilson

Wilson has shown that he can score and rebound in a difficult Chicago environment. He falls in the order without losing our confidence in his future. We want stronger defensive consistency and efficient possessions that translate into a more competitive team.

07

Meleek Thomas

Thomas moves up because his offensive tools merit a longer look. The challenge is translating individual skill into repeatable, winning contributions. His ranking is a 4DK editorial judgment pending the next full statistical audit.

08

Aday Mara

Mara enters the top ten as a size-and-interior-impact candidate. Rim protection, screening and finishing can change a game without a high scoring average. His minutes, matchups and ability to stay effective in space deserve close evaluation.

09

Keaton Wagler

Wagler remains in the top ten, but the margin is narrowing. Playing a useful role on a strong team matters; so does showing he can create an advantage rather than merely finish one. The next week will test the reliability of his contributions.

10

Brayden Burries

Burries wins the final spot in a tight debate. His scoring and rebounding give him an edge for our editorial board, though the outside shooting and efficiency need improvement. Kingston Flemings and Trevon Brazile remain serious challengers.

JUST OUTSIDE THE TEN

Kingston Flemings and Trevon Brazile. Flemings offers playmaking and efficiency; Brazile brings athletic finishing and complementary defensive value. Both are in the conversation for next week's board.
