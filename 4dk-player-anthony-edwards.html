The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Edwards has the athleticism, power and scoring package of the next great American perimeter superstar. Last season he averaged 28.8 points on 48.9 percent shooting and nearly 40 percent from three. That matters because the old version of the Ant conversation centered on whether the jumper would ever become efficient enough to match the explosiveness. It has.

The next step is control. The best players in the league do not only score 30; they dictate what the defense is allowed to do. Ant can still get baited into difficult pull-ups or spend stretches reacting to coverages instead of forcing the coverage he wants. The arrival of LaMelo Ball should help because Minnesota now has another elite passer who can organize possessions and get Ant the ball against a moving defense.

Defensively, Edwards has the tools to be a high-impact stopper when engaged. The question is whether he can sustain that effort while carrying an enormous scoring load. If the playmaking takes one more step and Minnesota becomes a true title team, Ant has an MVP path. The talent is already there; the final leap is about commanding games from start to finish.
