THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Amen’s physical profile is already special: big guard size, elite burst, rebounding, point-of-attack defense and the ability to turn steals or long rebounds into instant transition offense. Playing 79 games while averaging 18.3 points, 7.8 rebounds and 5.3 assists showed that he is no longer just a projection. He is already impacting winning. The jumper remains the swing skill. At 21.6 percent from three last season, teams can still load the paint and change the geometry of Houston’s offense. Even with that weakness, his defense and rim pressure make him one of the league’s most valuable young two-way players.
