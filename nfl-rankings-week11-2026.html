Around the league

What changed on Sunday and Monday.

Two paths to 8–1

Buffalo needed eleven completions to beat the Jets. San Francisco needed 46 points to get out of Dallas. We trust Buffalo’s ability to win a game it doesn’t throw particularly well in. We trust the 49ers to find a mismatch when everybody in the building knows they need a score. Minnesota gets the next crack at that distinction on Sunday night.

The cost of a bye

Denver and Chicago entered the weekend with seven wins and kept every one of them. Both now occupy wild-card positions. Denver gets the Raiders next. Chicago gets the Saints. Even a win in those games cannot erase the division-record advantage Kansas City holds or the head-to-head result Minnesota already owns against Chicago.

Monday belonged to Hampton

For the Chargers, the back’s 105 yards turned a twelve-completion night from Herbert into a road win. For Baltimore, the inverse hurt more. Brissett completed 33 passes and still couldn’t compensate for a team rushing total of sixteen. A home loss to a winless opponent leaves Baltimore two wins behind even the third-place Browns in its own division.
