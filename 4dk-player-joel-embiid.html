The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Embiid’s ranking is shaped by health more than talent. Even in a season limited to 38 games, he still averaged 26.9 points and shot 85.4 percent from the line. A healthy Embiid remains one of the most difficult players in the league to guard because he can score from the post, face up, draw fouls and anchor a defense at the other end. Philadelphia’s new roster reduces how much he has to carry every night, which could be the best thing for him. The question is no longer whether he can dominate. It is whether his body can hold up through the regular season and then survive four playoff rounds. If the answer is yes, No. 27 is far too low.
