THE 4DK READ

San Francisco remains the NFC West’s pace-setter, but its unbeaten aura is gone. Kansas City is still positioned near the top of the AFC West despite a harsh 20–3 loss in Seattle. Cleveland’s 27–24 win over Tennessee keeps pressure on the AFC North. The NFC North race has its own tension after Detroit edged Green Bay, 20–17, while Minnesota continues its unbeaten start.

The NFC East cannot be closed out before Dallas–Philadelphia on Monday. And Tampa Bay’s blowout of Carolina demands a fresh NFC South tiebreaker check before we declare a definitive leader. The important story is not just the current record—it is how these divisions are starting to separate.
