THE 4DK READ

San Francisco remains the NFC West’s pace-setter, but its unbeaten aura is gone. Kansas City is still positioned near the top of the AFC West despite a harsh 20–3 loss in Seattle. Cleveland’s 27–24 win over Tennessee keeps pressure on the AFC North. The NFC North race has its own tension after Detroit edged Green Bay, 20–17, while Minnesota remains unbeaten at 6–0.

Dallas closed Week 7 with a 38–21 road win over Philadelphia. The Cowboys move to 3–4, while Philadelphia falls to 2–5. The Giants lead the NFC East at 4–2–1. Carolina remains atop the NFC South at 3–3 despite Tampa Bay’s 43–13 win. The important story is not just the current record—it is how these divisions are starting to separate.
