The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Ja at 40 is going to make people mad, and that is understandable. The explosiveness, passing and star-level creation are still obvious. The problem is that he played only 20 games last season, shot 41 percent from the field and 23.5 percent from three, then had another season end early. Portland is betting that a new environment can reset the trajectory. If Ja is available, aggressive and gets back to turning paint pressure into efficient offense, he is not a No. 40 player. Until he proves it over a full season again, the ranking has to reflect the uncertainty.
