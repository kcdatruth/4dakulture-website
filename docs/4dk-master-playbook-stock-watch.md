### Core editorial requirements
1. **Deep, original analysis for every entry.** No thin or repetitive filler. Each player/team deserves a substantial, independently written feature covering the baseline, current trajectory, tactical role, surrounding context, fair counterargument, and forward outlook. Issue 03's expanded long-form treatment is the minimum editorial reference.
2. **One unique 4DK Watchpoint per entry.** End each feature with a clearly titled, individual, forward-looking question or verdict. It must be about that player's actual team, role, injury situation, organizational stakes, and likely next decision. Never use a generic reusable watchpoint.
3. **Movement versus expectations.** Stock Up/Down reflects meaningful change relative to recent performance and reasonable role expectations, not absolute talent, team record alone, or reputation. Account for playing time, usage, efficiency, injuries, sample size and roster context. Do not force names to fill a quota.
4. **Verify before publishing.** Check team affiliation, injuries, dates, game results, player numbers and comparative stats against authoritative current sources. Label the statistical cutoff date; avoid invented precision or outdated teams. Do not claim an image is working merely because its URL is inserted.
5. **Individualized basketball questions.** Examine tactical responsibilities, minutes, roster competition, coaching choices, contract or trade outlook where grounded, and the team's ceiling. Develop storylines across editions rather than reusing boilerplate.
6. **Premium 4DK presentation.** Mobile-first black background, oversized cream headings, gold metadata and scores, red dividers, legible long-form body text, and verified player headshots/team logos. Keep visual polish subordinate to substantive writing.
7. **Complete editorial structure.** Stock Up, Stock Down and Stock To Watch; substantial analysis and a personalized watchpoint for every selection; prominent headline and edition date; useful jump links; historical comparisons when verified. Issue 03's 10 Up / 10 Down / 3 To Watch is the reference format, not a requirement to invent filler.
8. **Ongoing storylines.** Revisit prior Stock To Watch entries and previously featured names when there is new evidence. Maintain a candidate watchlist without automatically promoting players or teams.
9. **Immutable archives and single latest link.** Publish each issue at its own dated URL; preserve every past issue intact. Point the main homepage and NBA headquarters to the same latest issue; keep archives navigable.
10. **Low-friction publishing.** Prefer direct GitHub commits/PRs to `kcdatruth/4dakulture-website`, avoiding repeated ZIP extraction and manual uploads. Preserve all unrelated content. Verify actual deployment and mobile rendering before calling a release fully QA-complete.
