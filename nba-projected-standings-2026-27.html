WHO RUNS THE LEAGUE?

Our preseason East and West board, built from the full 30-team 4 Da Kulture season preview. Records, seeds and the reasoning behind every spot.

By Kcdatruth • 4 Da Kulture • September 2026

The East is deep enough that 48 wins may only get you the 8 seed. Out West, OKC and San Antonio set a brutal pace while Minnesota and Luka's Lakers crash the top four.
