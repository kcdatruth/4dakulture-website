4 Da Kulture • Week 10 • November 10, 2026

The Power 32

Editorial review only — do not publish. Each team's Week 9 result is verified against the NFL schedule. Rankings are 4DK editorial choices. Player stats, injuries, last-five results, previous rank movement and full standings still require verification. These are first-pass analyses, not final long-form entries.

No. 1 • Week 9: 23–6 over Minnesota

Buffalo Bills

A prime-time defensive statement reinforced Buffalo's case for the top line. Holding a six-win opponent to six points matters as much as the final margin; the next test is sustaining that discipline when opponents force a higher-scoring game.

No. 2 • Week 9: 38–17 over Las Vegas

San Francisco 49ers

San Francisco answered a divisional neighbor's challenge with a 21-point win. The offense showed finishing power, and a 7–1 record keeps the 49ers on Buffalo's heels; the question is whether this balance survives a tougher defensive matchup.

No. 3 • Week 9: 26–21 over Carolina

Denver Broncos

Five consecutive wins speak to resilience, but the five-point escape against a three-win Carolina team is a reminder that Denver's margins remain tight. The Broncos belong near the top while still having to prove they can separate consistently.

No. 4 • Week 9: 33–10 over Baltimore

Jacksonville Jaguars

Jacksonville delivered arguably the week's clearest contender statement, dismantling Baltimore on Thursday. A 23-point margin against a team with postseason expectations validates this ranking beyond win-loss totals.

No. 5 • Week 9: 35–12 over the Jets

Kansas City Chiefs

The Chiefs took care of a struggling opponent by 23 points, exactly what an established contender should do. The larger question is how much this result tells us about their ceiling against the AFC's best.

No. 6 • Week 9: 27–24 over Tampa Bay

Chicago Bears

Chicago's seventh win arrived despite four Caleb Williams interceptions, a result that illustrates both its toughness and its volatility. Surviving is impressive; repeating that turnover profile against elite competition is unsustainable.

No. 7 • Week 9: Bye week

Pittsburgh Steelers

Pittsburgh entered the break with a four-game winning streak and a 6–2 record. Rest helps, but the next outing must demonstrate that the momentum is real rather than merely a favorable run of results.

No. 8 • Week 9: 19–9 over Miami

Detroit Lions

Detroit won with defense and control instead of a fireworks show. Limiting Miami to nine points is a useful reminder that a contender needs multiple winning scripts, particularly when its offense isn't explosive.

No. 9 • Week 9: 34–17 over Atlanta

Cincinnati Bengals

Cincinnati made its Madrid appearance count, doubling Atlanta's point total and stretching its winning streak to four. The Bengals are increasingly credible, but their position in a crowded AFC North leaves little room for a stumble.

No. 10 • Week 9: 45–27 over New England

Green Bay Packers

Forty-five points on the road against a five-win team makes this one of Week 9's most convincing offensive displays. Green Bay's rise is justified; the challenge is reproducing that efficiency when the game becomes a defensive grind.

No. 11 • Week 9: 6–23 loss to Buffalo

Minnesota Vikings

Minnesota's prime-time loss exposed the difference between a strong record and a fully functioning offense. Six points against Buffalo cannot be dismissed as an off night; the Vikings need a sharper response before confidence erodes.

No. 12 • Week 9: 19–11 over Philadelphia

New York Giants

The Giants continue to lead a turbulent NFC East and won a divisional road game without needing a shootout. The result strengthens their control of the division, even if the broader NFC contender conversation remains open.

No. 13 • Week 9: 20–13 over Washington

Los Angeles Rams

A seven-point road win kept the Rams above .500. Los Angeles handled its assignment, but the tight scoreline shows why this team is still chasing the NFC's top tier rather than dictating it.

No. 14 • Week 9: 17–3 over Arizona

Seattle Seahawks

Seattle's defense did the heavy lifting, allowing only a field goal. A low-scoring win is not a weakness when the opponent is kept out of the end zone, and the Seahawks remain firmly in the NFC West chase.

No. 15 • Week 9: 27–45 loss to Green Bay

New England Patriots

Giving up 45 points at home is a major warning sign for a club fighting to stay in the AFC picture. New England must restore defensive resistance quickly; offensive production alone will not cover that kind of leakage.

No. 16 • Week 9: 22–23 overtime loss to New Orleans

Cleveland Browns

Cleveland was one point from a needed road win and instead absorbed a damaging overtime defeat. At 5–4, close-game execution may determine whether this season becomes a playoff push or a missed opportunity.

No. 17 • Week 9: 27–23 over Indianapolis

Dallas Cowboys

Dallas secured a four-point road win and kept its NFC East pursuit alive. The Cowboys can build from the result, but a 4–5 record means moral victories are no longer enough.

No. 18 • Week 9: 44–22 over the Chargers

Houston Texans

Houston doubled up Los Angeles in its most emphatic offensive result of the week. The 44-point outburst is encouraging; consistency is the next hurdle for a team still two games under .500.

No. 19 • Week 9: 17–38 loss to San Francisco

Las Vegas Raiders

The Raiders were overmatched by a 49ers team with a clear identity. At 4–5, the path forward depends on reducing the gap against upper-tier opponents, not merely beating the teams around them.

No. 20 • Week 9: 10–33 loss to Jacksonville

Baltimore Ravens

A fourth consecutive defeat, capped by a 23-point home loss, puts Baltimore's season at a crossroads. The Ravens need answers on both sides of the ball before the AFC North race slips further away.

No. 21 • Week 9: 21–26 loss to Denver

Carolina Panthers

Carolina pushed one of the AFC's hottest teams to the final margin, a credible effort even in defeat. The Panthers need to turn competitiveness into wins; their record offers little cushion.

No. 22 • Week 9: 23–22 overtime win over Cleveland

New Orleans Saints

New Orleans delivered Week 9's notable upset, edging Cleveland in overtime. The victory is a meaningful confidence builder, but one dramatic finish does not erase the need for week-to-week consistency.

No. 23 • Week 9: 24–27 loss to Chicago

Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Tampa Bay had opportunities against a Chicago team that gave the ball away four times. Failing to turn those chances into a road win underlines why the Buccaneers are still searching for stability.

No. 24 • Week 9: 17–34 loss to Cincinnati

Atlanta Falcons

Atlanta's international-stage defeat exposed the distance between its current execution and a surging Bengals team. A 3–6 record makes the second half of the season about urgency rather than patience.

No. 25 • Week 9: 3–17 loss to Seattle

Arizona Cardinals

Arizona managed only three points in Seattle, an offensive performance that offers little room for optimism. Defensive competitiveness means little without a reliable way to sustain drives and score.

No. 26 • Week 9: 13–20 loss to the Rams

Washington Commanders

Washington stayed within one score but could not finish the comeback. A tie already complicates the standings picture, and the Commanders now need results—not just competitive stretches—to recover.

No. 27 • Week 9: 9–19 loss to Detroit

Miami Dolphins

Miami's offense produced only nine points at home. The Dolphins need a reset in execution and consistency before discussing any realistic turnaround from a one-win start.

No. 28 • Week 9: 12–35 loss to Kansas City

New York Jets

A 23-point defeat in Kansas City again exposed the Jets' inability to stay with top opponents. The focus should be on establishing a sustainable offensive baseline and reducing lopsided losses.

No. 29 • Week 9: 23–27 loss to Dallas

Indianapolis Colts

Indianapolis stayed close but came away empty-handed. At 2–7, narrow defeats count the same as blowouts; the Colts must convert competitive games into actual wins.

No. 30 • Week 9: Bye week

Tennessee Titans

Tennessee used the week off at 1–7, with the standings already demanding a longer-term view. The next stretch should be judged on growth, execution and evidence of a dependable foundation.

No. 31 • Week 9: 11–19 loss to the Giants

Philadelphia Eagles

A divisional home loss dropped Philadelphia to 2–7 and deepened its free fall. Eleven points against a rival is an unacceptable offensive return for a club trying to salvage its season.

No. 32 • Week 9: 22–44 loss to Houston

Los Angeles Chargers

The Chargers remain winless after surrendering 44 points at home. This is no longer about isolated mistakes; Los Angeles needs structural improvement across the roster to avoid a historically bleak season.
