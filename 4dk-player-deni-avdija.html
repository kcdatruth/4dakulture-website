THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Avdija has grown into the kind of big, physical wing every team wants. Last season’s 24.2 points and 6.7 assists represented a major offensive leap, and his rebounding, transition play and ability to absorb contact make him useful in almost any lineup. Portland can use him as a handler, screener, secondary creator or mismatch attacker. The next question is efficiency from deep: 31.8 percent from three allowed defenses to shade away from him more than you want for a primary wing creator. If the jumper rises, his all-around game is strong enough to push him toward All-Star territory.
