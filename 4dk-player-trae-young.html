The 4DK scouting report

THE GAME BEHIND THE NUMBERS.

Trae remains one of basketball’s best offensive engines, but last season gave us only 15 games. The range, passing imagination and pick-and-roll command still create efficient offense almost by themselves. In Washington, he will have the ball and freedom to organize everything, and that makes his health especially important. The efficiency in a tiny sample was fine, but the volume was lower than his Atlanta peak. The defense will always cap some lineups, so the offensive value has to be overwhelming. If he plays 70 games and gets back near his normal scoring level while keeping the assists around double digits, he can climb this list quickly.
